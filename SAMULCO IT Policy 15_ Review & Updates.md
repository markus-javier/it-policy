## **1. PURPOSE**

This policy establishes the framework for regularly reviewing, updating,
and maintaining all Information Technology policies at Sta. Ana
Multipurpose Cooperative (SAMULCO). It ensures that IT policies remain
current, relevant, and effective in addressing evolving business needs,
regulatory requirements, and technology changes while maintaining
consistency with organizational objectives.

## **2. SCOPE**

This policy applies to all:

-   SAMULCO IT policies and procedures

-   Policy development and maintenance processes

-   Personnel responsible for policy management

-   Stakeholders involved in policy review and approval

-   Systems and processes for policy distribution and implementation

-   Documentation and version control procedures

## **3. POLICY LIFECYCLE MANAGEMENT**

### **3.1 Policy Lifecycle Phases**

#### **3.1.1 Policy Development Phase**

**Initial Policy Creation:**

-   **Needs Assessment:** Identification of policy requirements and
    > business needs

-   **Stakeholder Consultation:** Engagement with relevant stakeholders
    > and subject matter experts

-   **Research and Analysis:** Industry best practices and regulatory
    > requirement research

-   **Draft Development:** Initial policy draft creation and internal
    > review

-   **Impact Assessment:** Analysis of policy impact on operations and
    > resources

**Development Process:**

1.  **Business Case:** Clear business justification for new policy
    > requirement

2.  **Resource Planning:** Identification of resources needed for policy
    > development

3.  **Team Assembly:** Assignment of policy development team and subject
    > matter experts

4.  **Timeline Establishment:** Development schedule and milestone
    > planning

5.  **Quality Standards:** Application of policy development standards
    > and templates

#### **3.1.2 Policy Approval Phase**

**Review and Approval Process:**

-   **Technical Review:** ICT Department technical accuracy and
    > feasibility review

-   **Legal Review:** Legal counsel review for compliance and liability
    > considerations

-   **Business Review:** Department head review for operational impact
    > and feasibility

-   **Management Review:** Executive management review and preliminary
    > approval

-   **Board Approval:** Board of Directors final review and formal
    > approval

**Approval Documentation:**

-   **Review Comments:** Documentation of all review feedback and
    > resolutions

-   **Approval Records:** Formal approval documentation and
    > authorization

-   **Version Control:** Initial version numbering and document control
    > establishment

-   **Effective Date:** Official policy effective date and
    > implementation timeline

-   **Distribution List:** Identification of personnel requiring policy
    > awareness

#### **3.1.3 Policy Implementation Phase**

**Implementation Planning:**

-   **Communication Strategy:** Plan for communicating policy to all
    > affected personnel

-   **Training Requirements:** Identification of training needs and
    > delivery methods

-   **System Updates:** Technical system changes required for policy
    > enforcement

-   **Process Integration:** Integration with existing business
    > processes and procedures

-   **Compliance Monitoring:** Establishment of compliance monitoring
    > and measurement

**Implementation Activities:**

-   **Policy Publication:** Official publication and distribution of
    > approved policy

-   **Training Delivery:** Delivery of required training and awareness
    > programs

-   **System Configuration:** Technical implementation of policy
    > controls and requirements

-   **Process Updates:** Update of related procedures and work
    > instructions

-   **Compliance Verification:** Initial verification of policy
    > implementation and adherence

#### **3.1.4 Policy Maintenance Phase**

**Ongoing Management:**

-   **Regular Monitoring:** Continuous monitoring of policy
    > effectiveness and compliance

-   **Stakeholder Feedback:** Collection and analysis of user feedback
    > and suggestions

-   **Performance Assessment:** Evaluation of policy performance against
    > objectives

-   **Environmental Scanning:** Monitoring of external changes affecting
    > policy relevance

-   **Issue Resolution:** Address implementation issues and
    > clarification requests

**Maintenance Activities:**

-   **Minor Updates:** Small corrections, clarifications, and
    > administrative changes

-   **Compliance Monitoring:** Ongoing assessment of policy compliance
    > and effectiveness

-   **Training Updates:** Refresher training and awareness activities

-   **Documentation Maintenance:** Maintenance of accurate and current
    > documentation

-   **Stakeholder Communication:** Regular communication with policy
    > stakeholders

#### **3.1.5 Policy Retirement Phase**

**Retirement Criteria:**

-   **Obsolescence:** Policy no longer relevant due to technology or
    > business changes

-   **Supersession:** Policy replaced by new or updated policy
    > addressing same requirements

-   **Regulatory Changes:** Policy requirements eliminated by regulatory
    > or legal changes

-   **Business Process Changes:** Underlying business processes no
    > longer require policy

-   **Consolidation:** Policy content merged into other existing
    > policies

**Retirement Process:**

-   **Impact Assessment:** Analysis of policy retirement impact on
    > operations and compliance

-   **Stakeholder Notification:** Communication of policy retirement to
    > affected personnel

-   **Transition Planning:** Plan for transitioning from retired policy
    > to new requirements

-   **Archive Management:** Proper archival of retired policy for
    > historical reference

-   **Documentation Updates:** Update of related documentation and
    > cross-references

### **3.2 Version Control and Document Management**

#### **3.2.1 Version Control System**

**Version Numbering:**

-   **Major Versions:** Significant policy changes requiring full review
    > and approval (1.0, 2.0, 3.0)

-   **Minor Versions:** Minor updates, corrections, or clarifications
    > (1.1, 1.2, 1.3)

-   **Administrative Updates:** Administrative changes not affecting
    > policy substance (1.1a, 1.1b)

-   **Draft Versions:** Working drafts during development process
    > (1.0-DRAFT, 2.0-DRAFT)

-   **Emergency Updates:** Immediate updates pending formal review
    > (1.1-EMERGENCY)

**Change Documentation:**

-   **Change Log:** Detailed record of all changes made to each policy
    > version

-   **Rationale Documentation:** Explanation of reasons for each policy
    > change

-   **Impact Analysis:** Assessment of change impact on operations and
    > stakeholders

-   **Approval Records:** Documentation of approval authority for each
    > change level

-   **Distribution Records:** Record of policy distribution and
    > notification activities

#### **3.2.2 Document Control Standards**

**Document Format:**

-   **Standardized Template:** Consistent format and structure for all
    > IT policies

-   **Required Sections:** Standard sections including purpose, scope,
    > and approval signatures

-   **Formatting Guidelines:** Consistent formatting, fonts, and style
    > requirements

-   **Accessibility Standards:** Compliance with accessibility
    > requirements for all users

-   **Electronic Format:** Primary distribution in electronic format
    > with print capability

**Metadata Management:**

-   **Document Properties:** Comprehensive metadata including creation
    > date, author, and version

-   **Classification Marking:** Appropriate classification and handling
    > markings

-   **Distribution Information:** Record of authorized recipients and
    > distribution lists

-   **Review Schedule:** Next scheduled review date and responsible
    > parties

-   **Related Documents:** Cross-references to related policies and
    > procedures

### **3.3 Change Management Process**

#### **3.3.1 Change Request Process**

**Change Initiation:**

-   **Change Request Form:** Standardized form for requesting policy
    > changes

-   **Business Justification:** Clear explanation of need for policy
    > change

-   **Impact Assessment:** Initial assessment of change impact on
    > operations

-   **Resource Requirements:** Identification of resources needed for
    > change implementation

-   **Stakeholder Identification:** List of stakeholders affected by
    > proposed change

**Change Request Sources:**

-   **Internal Stakeholders:** Employees, management, and department
    > heads

-   **Regulatory Changes:** Updates required by regulatory or legal
    > changes

-   **Technology Changes:** Updates needed due to technology evolution
    > or upgrades

-   **Business Process Changes:** Changes driven by business process
    > improvements

-   **External Requirements:** Changes required by vendors, partners, or
    > customers

-   **Audit Findings:** Changes recommended by internal or external
    > audits

-   **Incident Lessons Learned:** Changes based on security incidents or
    > operational issues

#### **3.3.2 Change Assessment Process**

**Initial Assessment:**

-   **Change Categorization:** Classification of change as major, minor,
    > or administrative

-   **Priority Assignment:** Priority level based on urgency and
    > business impact

-   **Resource Assessment:** Evaluation of resources required for change
    > implementation

-   **Timeline Estimation:** Estimated time for change development and
    > implementation

-   **Risk Assessment:** Identification and evaluation of risks
    > associated with change

**Detailed Analysis:**

-   **Stakeholder Impact:** Detailed analysis of impact on all affected
    > stakeholders

-   **Technical Feasibility:** Assessment of technical feasibility and
    > implementation requirements

-   **Compliance Implications:** Analysis of regulatory and legal
    > compliance impact

-   **Cost-Benefit Analysis:** Evaluation of costs and benefits of
    > proposed change

-   **Alternative Options:** Consideration of alternative approaches to
    > address requirements

#### **3.3.3 Change Approval Process**

**Approval Authority Levels:**

-   **Administrative Changes:** ICT Manager approval for minor
    > corrections and clarifications

-   **Minor Policy Changes:** Department head and ICT Manager approval

-   **Significant Changes:** General Manager approval for substantial
    > policy modifications

-   **Major Policy Changes:** Executive management and Board committee
    > approval

-   **New Policies:** Board of Directors approval for all new policy
    > creation

**Change Control Board:**

-   **Membership:** Representatives from MIS, affected departments,
    > legal, and management

-   **Responsibilities:** Review and approval of significant policy
    > changes

-   **Meeting Frequency:** Monthly meetings with ad-hoc meetings for
    > urgent changes

-   **Decision Process:** Consensus-based decision making with
    > escalation procedures

-   **Documentation:** Formal documentation of all change decisions and
    > rationale

## **4. REVIEW FREQUENCY AND SCHEDULING**

### **4.1 Regular Review Schedule**

#### **4.1.1 Annual Policy Review**

**Comprehensive Annual Review:**

-   **Schedule:** Complete review of all IT policies annually by fiscal
    > year end

-   **Scope:** Comprehensive review of policy content, effectiveness,
    > and relevance

-   **Participants:** All policy stakeholders, subject matter experts,
    > and management

-   **Deliverables:** Annual policy review report with recommendations
    > for updates

-   **Timeline:** 90-day review period with 30 days for implementation
    > planning

**Annual Review Process:**

1.  **Review Planning:** Develop annual review plan and schedule

2.  **Stakeholder Engagement:** Engage all relevant stakeholders in
    > review process

3.  **Content Assessment:** Systematic review of each policy for
    > currency and effectiveness

4.  **Gap Analysis:** Identification of policy gaps and coverage areas
    > needing attention

5.  **Recommendations:** Development of specific recommendations for
    > policy improvements

6.  **Priority Planning:** Prioritization of recommended changes and
    > improvements

7.  **Implementation Planning:** Development of implementation plan for
    > approved changes

#### **4.1.2 Ongoing Monitoring Schedule**

**Quarterly Reviews:**

-   **Scope:** Limited review focusing on high-priority policies and
    > recent changes

-   **Focus Areas:** Compliance metrics, implementation issues, and
    > stakeholder feedback

-   **Participants:** Policy owners, ICT management, and key
    > stakeholders

-   **Deliverables:** Quarterly policy status report and immediate
    > action items

-   **Timeline:** 30-day review period with immediate implementation of
    > minor changes

**Monthly Assessments:**

-   **Scope:** Monitoring of policy effectiveness and compliance metrics

-   **Focus Areas:** Performance indicators, compliance rates, and issue
    > identification

-   **Participants:** ICT team, compliance officers, and department
    > representatives

-   **Deliverables:** Monthly compliance dashboard and exception reports

-   **Timeline:** Ongoing monitoring with monthly reporting cycle

### **4.2 Triggered Reviews**

#### **4.2.1 Event-Driven Reviews**

**Regulatory Changes:**

-   **Trigger:** New or modified regulations affecting IT operations

-   **Timeline:** Review within 30 days of regulatory change publication

-   **Scope:** All policies potentially affected by regulatory change

-   **Process:** Expedited review process with legal and compliance
    > involvement

-   **Outcome:** Policy updates to ensure continued regulatory
    > compliance

**Security Incidents:**

-   **Trigger:** Major security incidents or breaches requiring policy
    > assessment

-   **Timeline:** Review within 15 days of incident resolution

-   **Scope:** Policies related to incident cause and response
    > effectiveness

-   **Process:** Incident-specific review with security team and
    > affected stakeholders

-   **Outcome:** Policy enhancements to prevent similar incidents and
    > improve response

**Technology Changes:**

-   **Trigger:** Major technology implementations or infrastructure
    > changes

-   **Timeline:** Review concurrent with technology implementation
    > planning

-   **Scope:** Policies affected by new technology capabilities or
    > requirements

-   **Process:** Technical review with IT team and business stakeholders

-   **Outcome:** Policy updates to address new technology risks and
    > opportunities

#### **4.2.2 Stakeholder-Requested Reviews**

**Management Request:**

-   **Authority:** Executive management or Board of Directors request

-   **Priority:** High priority with expedited review process

-   **Timeline:** 30-60 days depending on scope and complexity

-   **Resources:** Dedicated resources assigned for requested review

-   **Reporting:** Direct reporting to requesting authority with
    > recommendations

**Department Request:**

-   **Authority:** Department heads or operational managers

-   **Priority:** Standard priority within normal review cycle

-   **Timeline:** 60-90 days for completion of review process

-   **Coordination:** Coordination with requesting department throughout
    > process

-   **Implementation:** Department involvement in implementation
    > planning and execution

**External Stakeholder Request:**

-   **Authority:** Regulatory authorities, auditors, or business
    > partners

-   **Priority:** High priority with appropriate response timeline

-   **Timeline:** Based on external stakeholder requirements and
    > deadlines

-   **Coordination:** Legal and management involvement in response
    > coordination

-   **Documentation:** Comprehensive documentation of review process and
    > outcomes

### **4.3 Review Prioritization**

#### **4.3.1 Priority Assessment Criteria**

**Business Impact:**

-   **Critical Policies:** Policies affecting core business operations
    > and member services

-   **High-Impact Policies:** Policies with significant operational or
    > financial impact

-   **Medium-Impact Policies:** Policies affecting specific departments
    > or processes

-   **Low-Impact Policies:** Administrative or support policies with
    > limited impact

-   **Support Policies:** Policies supporting other policies or
    > providing background information

**Risk Level:**

-   **High-Risk Areas:** Policies addressing high-risk technology and
    > operational areas

-   **Regulatory Risk:** Policies required for regulatory compliance and
    > examination

-   **Security Risk:** Policies critical for information security and
    > data protection

-   **Operational Risk:** Policies essential for business continuity and
    > operational stability

-   **Reputation Risk:** Policies protecting organizational reputation
    > and member trust

#### **4.3.2 Review Resource Allocation**

**Resource Assignment:**

-   **Dedicated Resources:** Full-time assignment for critical policy
    > reviews

-   **Shared Resources:** Part-time assignment for standard policy
    > reviews

-   **External Resources:** Outside expertise for specialized or complex
    > policy areas

-   **Cross-Functional Teams:** Multi-departmental teams for policies
    > affecting multiple areas

-   **Management Oversight:** Senior management involvement in
    > high-priority reviews

**Budget Allocation:**

-   **Review Budget:** Annual budget allocation for policy review and
    > update activities

-   **Emergency Budget:** Reserve budget for urgent or unplanned policy
    > reviews

-   **External Costs:** Budget for external legal, consulting, or
    > technical expertise

-   **Training Costs:** Budget for training related to policy updates
    > and changes

-   **Technology Costs:** Budget for systems and tools supporting policy
    > management

## **5. APPROVAL PROCESS FOR UPDATES**

### **5.1 Approval Authority Matrix**

#### **5.1.1 Change Classification and Authority**

**Administrative Changes:**

-   **Authority:** ICT Manager

-   **Examples:** Typographical corrections, formatting updates, contact
    > information changes

-   **Process:** Direct approval with documentation in change log

-   **Timeline:** Immediate implementation upon approval

-   **Notification:** Email notification to policy stakeholders

**Minor Policy Changes:**

-   **Authority:** Department Head + ICT Manager

-   **Examples:** Clarifications, minor procedure updates,
    > non-substantive changes

-   **Process:** Joint approval with impact assessment and stakeholder
    > notification

-   **Timeline:** 5-10 business days from approval to implementation

-   **Documentation:** Change request form and approval documentation

**Significant Policy Changes:**

-   **Authority:** General Manager + Department Head + ICT Manager

-   **Examples:** New procedures, significant content changes,
    > technology requirement updates

-   **Process:** Management review with business case and implementation
    > plan

-   **Timeline:** 15-30 business days from approval to implementation

-   **Communication:** Formal communication to all affected stakeholders

**Major Policy Changes:**

-   **Authority:** Board Committee + Executive Management

-   **Examples:** Fundamental policy changes, new policy areas,
    > strategic direction changes

-   **Process:** Board committee review with comprehensive impact
    > analysis

-   **Timeline:** 30-60 business days from approval to implementation

-   **Board Reporting:** Formal reporting to full Board of Directors

**New Policy Creation:**

-   **Authority:** Board of Directors

-   **Examples:** Entirely new policies, major regulatory compliance
    > policies

-   **Process:** Full Board review and approval with legal and
    > compliance review

-   **Timeline:** 60-90 business days from approval to implementation

-   **Documentation:** Comprehensive policy development documentation
    > and business case

#### **5.1.2 Escalation Procedures**

**Authority Escalation:**

-   **Disagreement Resolution:** Escalation process for disagreements on
    > policy changes

-   **Emergency Approval:** Accelerated approval process for urgent
    > policy updates

-   **Resource Conflicts:** Escalation for resource allocation and
    > priority disputes

-   **Technical Issues:** Escalation for complex technical or
    > implementation issues

-   **Compliance Concerns:** Escalation for regulatory or legal
    > compliance questions

**Escalation Timeline:**

-   **Level 1:** Departmental resolution within 5 business days

-   **Level 2:** Management resolution within 10 business days

-   **Level 3:** Executive resolution within 15 business days

-   **Level 4:** Board committee resolution within 30 business days

-   **Emergency:** Same-day resolution for critical issues

### **5.2 Review and Approval Process**

#### **5.2.1 Multi-Stage Review Process**

**Stage 1 - Initial Review:**

-   **Technical Review:** ICT Department review for technical accuracy
    > and feasibility

-   **Content Review:** Subject matter expert review for accuracy and
    > completeness

-   **Format Review:** Review for compliance with documentation
    > standards and templates

-   **Cross-Reference Review:** Verification of consistency with related
    > policies and procedures

-   **Quality Assurance:** Editorial review for clarity, grammar, and
    > readability

**Stage 2 - Stakeholder Review:**

-   **Business Impact Review:** Assessment of impact on business
    > operations and processes

-   **User Review:** Review by end users and affected personnel for
    > practicality

-   **Department Review:** Review by affected departments for
    > operational feasibility

-   **Management Review:** Management assessment of resource and
    > strategic implications

-   **Legal Review:** Legal counsel review for compliance and liability
    > considerations

**Stage 3 - Final Review:**

-   **Executive Review:** Senior management review and preliminary
    > approval

-   **Board Review:** Board or committee review for significant changes

-   **Compliance Review:** Final compliance and regulatory adherence
    > verification

-   **Implementation Review:** Review of implementation plan and
    > resource allocation

-   **Communication Review:** Review of communication and training plans

#### **5.2.2 Review Documentation**

**Review Records:**

-   **Review Checklist:** Standardized checklist for each review stage

-   **Reviewer Comments:** Detailed comments and feedback from each
    > reviewer

-   **Issue Resolution:** Documentation of how review comments were
    > addressed

-   **Approval Sign-off:** Formal sign-off by each required approver

-   **Version History:** Complete history of review cycles and changes

**Decision Documentation:**

-   **Business Case:** Justification for policy changes and updates

-   **Impact Analysis:** Assessment of change impact on operations and
    > stakeholders

-   **Risk Assessment:** Evaluation of risks associated with policy
    > changes

-   **Cost-Benefit Analysis:** Analysis of costs and benefits of
    > proposed changes

-   **Implementation Plan:** Detailed plan for implementing approved
    > changes

### **5.3 Emergency Update Procedures**

#### **5.3.1 Emergency Criteria**

**Emergency Situations:**

-   **Security Threats:** Immediate security threats requiring policy
    > updates

-   **Regulatory Mandates:** New regulatory requirements with immediate
    > compliance deadlines

-   **System Failures:** Critical system failures requiring immediate
    > policy adjustments

    > modifications

    > approval

-   **Legal Counsel:** Authority to approve emergency updates for legal

    > risk management

    > situations

#### **5.3.2 Emergency Process**

**Immediate Response:**

-   **Situation Assessment:** Rapid assessment of emergency situation
    > and policy implications

-   **Authority Notification:** Immediate notification of appropriate
    > approval authority

-   **Emergency Approval:** Expedited approval process with abbreviated
    > review cycle

-   **Immediate Implementation:** Immediate implementation of emergency
    > policy updates

    > stakeholders and personnel

**Follow-up Process:**

-   **Post-Emergency Review:** Full review of emergency actions within

-   **Permanent Policy Update:** Development of permanent policy update
    > if needed

-   **Lessons Learned:** Analysis of emergency response effectiveness
    > and improvements

-   **Process Enhancement:** Updates to emergency procedures based on
    > experience

    > rationale

## **7. CHANGE DOCUMENTATION AND COMMUNICATION**

### **7.1 Documentation Standards**
