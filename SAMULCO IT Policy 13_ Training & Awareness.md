## **1. PURPOSE**

This policy establishes a comprehensive framework for IT security
training and awareness programs at Sta. Ana Multipurpose Cooperative
(SAMULCO). It ensures all personnel have the knowledge and skills
necessary to protect information assets, comply with security policies,
and respond appropriately to security threats and incidents.

## **2. SCOPE**

This policy applies to all:

-   SAMULCO employees (permanent, temporary, contractual)

-   Board members and officers

-   Third-party contractors and consultants

-   Vendors with access to SAMULCO systems

-   Volunteers and interns

-   All IT systems and security awareness programs

## **5. CYBERSECURITY AWARENESS PROGRAM**

### **5.1 Core Cybersecurity Training**

#### **5.1.1 Fundamental Security Concepts**

**Information Security Principles:**

-   **Confidentiality:** Protecting information from unauthorized
    > disclosure

-   **Integrity:** Ensuring information accuracy and completeness

-   **Availability:** Maintaining reliable access to information and
    > systems

-   **Authentication:** Verifying identity before granting access

-   **Authorization:** Controlling what users can access and do

-   **Accountability:** Tracking and monitoring user activities

**Security Risk Awareness:**

-   **Threat Landscape:** Current and emerging cyber threats

-   **Vulnerability Management:** Common system and application
    > vulnerabilities

-   **Risk Assessment:** Understanding and evaluating security risks

-   **Impact Analysis:** Consequences of security incidents and breaches

-   **Mitigation Strategies:** Approaches to reduce and manage security
    > risks

#### **5.1.2 Technology Security**

**System Security:**

-   **Operating System Security:** Secure configuration and maintenance

-   **Application Security:** Safe software use and configuration

-   **Network Security:** Understanding network threats and protections

-   **Mobile Device Security:** Secure use of smartphones and tablets

-   **Cloud Security:** Safe use of cloud services and applications

**Data Security:**

-   **Data Classification:** Understanding data sensitivity levels

-   **Data Handling:** Proper procedures for accessing and processing
    > data

-   **Data Storage:** Secure storage practices and requirements

-   **Data Transmission:** Safe methods for sending and receiving data

-   **Data Disposal:** Proper destruction and disposal procedures

### **5.2 Threat Recognition and Response**

#### **5.2.1 Common Cyber Threats**

**Malware Threats:**

-   **Viruses:** Self-replicating malicious code

-   **Worms:** Network-spreading malicious programs

-   **Trojans:** Disguised malicious software

-   **Ransomware:** Encryption-based extortion attacks

-   **Spyware:** Information-gathering malicious software

-   **Rootkits:** Stealth malware for persistent access

**Social Engineering Attacks:**

-   **Phishing:** Fraudulent emails to steal credentials or information

-   **Spear Phishing:** Targeted phishing attacks

-   **Vishing:** Voice-based social engineering attacks

-   **Smishing:** SMS/text message-based attacks

-   **Pretexting:** Fabricated scenarios to obtain information

-   **Baiting:** Enticing offers to compromise security

#### **5.2.2 Attack Recognition Skills**

**Email Security Awareness:**

-   **Suspicious Sender Identification:** Recognizing fraudulent email
    > addresses

-   **Phishing Indicator Recognition:** Identifying phishing attempt
    > characteristics

-   **Attachment Safety:** Safe handling of email attachments

-   **Link Verification:** Checking links before clicking

-   **Reporting Procedures:** Steps for reporting suspicious emails

**Web Security Awareness:**

-   **Safe Browsing Practices:** Avoiding malicious websites and content

-   **Download Safety:** Secure downloading and installation procedures

-   **Social Media Security:** Safe use of social media platforms

-   **Online Shopping Security:** Secure online transaction practices

-   **Public Wi-Fi Safety:** Risks and precautions for public network
    > use

### **5.3 Incident Response Training**

#### **5.3.1 Incident Identification**

**Incident Indicators:**

-   **System Performance Issues:** Unusual slowdowns or behavior

-   **Unexpected Network Activity:** Suspicious network connections or
    > traffic

-   **Unauthorized Access Attempts:** Failed login attempts or access
    > violations

-   **Data Anomalies:** Unexpected data changes or corruption

-   **Security Alert Notifications:** System-generated security warnings

**Escalation Procedures:**

-   **Initial Assessment:** Determining if an incident has occurred

-   **Reporting Chain:** Who to contact and how to report incidents

-   **Information Gathering:** What information to collect and document

-   **Containment Actions:** Immediate steps to limit incident impact

-   **Communication Guidelines:** What to say and not say about
    > incidents

#### **5.3.2 Response Procedures**

**Immediate Response Actions:**

-   **Isolation:** Disconnecting affected systems from the network

-   **Documentation:** Recording incident details and actions taken

-   **Notification:** Alerting appropriate personnel and authorities

-   **Preservation:** Protecting evidence and audit trails

-   **Communication:** Coordinating with response team and stakeholders

**Follow-up Activities:**

-   **Investigation Support:** Assisting with incident investigation

-   **Recovery Assistance:** Supporting system restoration efforts

-   **Lessons Learned:** Participating in post-incident reviews

-   **Process Improvement:** Contributing to procedure updates and
    > improvements

-   **Training Updates:** Incorporating new knowledge into training
    > programs

## **6. DATA PRIVACY TRAINING**

### **6.1 Privacy Awareness Foundation**

#### **6.1.1 Privacy Principles and Rights**

**Fundamental Privacy Principles:**

-   **Lawfulness and Fairness:** Processing data legally and
    > transparently

-   **Purpose Limitation:** Using data only for stated purposes

-   **Data Minimization:** Collecting only necessary data

-   **Accuracy:** Maintaining correct and up-to-date information

-   **Storage Limitation:** Retaining data only as long as necessary

-   **Security:** Protecting data with appropriate safeguards

**Data Subject Rights:**

-   **Right to Information:** Understanding data collection and use

-   **Right of Access:** Obtaining copies of personal data

-   **Right to Rectification:** Correcting inaccurate information

-   **Right to Erasure:** Requesting deletion of personal data

-   **Right to Data Portability:** Receiving data in portable format

-   **Right to Object:** Objecting to certain types of processing

#### **6.1.2 Philippine Data Privacy Act Compliance**

**Legal Requirements:**

-   **Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173):** Key provisions and
    > requirements

-   **Implementing Rules and Regulations:** Detailed compliance
    > obligations

-   **National Privacy Commission Guidelines:** Regulatory guidance and
    > updates

-   **Sector-Specific Requirements:** Banking and financial services
    > obligations

-   **Cross-Border Transfer Rules:** International data transfer
    > restrictions

**Compliance Obligations:**

-   **Consent Management:** Obtaining and managing valid consent

-   **Data Processing Agreements:** Third-party data sharing
    > requirements

-   **Privacy Impact Assessments:** Evaluating privacy risks and impacts

-   **Breach Notification:** Reporting data breaches to authorities and
    > individuals

-   **Record Keeping:** Maintaining data processing activity records

### **6.2 Data Handling Procedures**

#### **6.2.1 Personal Data Processing**

**Data Collection:**

-   **Collection Principles:** Fair and transparent data collection
    > practices

-   **Consent Requirements:** When and how to obtain valid consent

-   **Collection Limitation:** Collecting only necessary and relevant
    > data

-   **Notice Provision:** Informing individuals about data collection
    > and use

-   **Special Categories:** Extra protections for sensitive personal
    > data

**Data Use and Sharing:**

-   **Purpose Limitation:** Using data only for stated purposes

-   **Legitimate Interests:** Balancing organizational needs with
    > privacy rights

-   **Third-Party Sharing:** Requirements for sharing data with external
    > parties

-   **Cross-Border Transfers:** Restrictions on international data
    > transfers

-   **Data Retention:** Keeping data only as long as necessary

#### **6.2.2 Privacy by Design Implementation**

**System and Process Design:**

-   **Privacy Considerations:** Integrating privacy into system design

-   **Data Protection Controls:** Building in appropriate safeguards

-   **Access Controls:** Limiting access to personal data

-   **Audit Trails:** Maintaining records of data processing activities

-   **Impact Assessments:** Evaluating privacy risks of new systems and
    > processes

**Operational Procedures:**

-   **Privacy Procedures:** Day-to-day privacy protection procedures

-   **Data Subject Requests:** Handling individual rights requests

-   **Incident Response:** Responding to privacy breaches and incidents

-   **Training and Awareness:** Ongoing privacy education and awareness

-   **Monitoring and Review:** Regular privacy compliance assessments

### **6.3 Member Data Protection**

#### **6.3.1 Member Information Handling**

**Member Data Categories:**

-   **Personal Information:** Names, addresses, contact information

-   **Financial Information:** Account numbers, transaction history,
    > credit information

-   **Sensitive Information:** Government IDs, biometric data, health
    > information

-   **Communication Records:** Emails, call logs, correspondence

-   **Behavioral Data:** Usage patterns, preferences, analytics data

**Protection Requirements:**

-   **Access Controls:** Limiting access to authorized personnel only

-   **Encryption:** Protecting data in transit and at rest

-   **Audit Logging:** Tracking access to and use of member data

-   **Data Minimization:** Using only necessary data for business
    > purposes

-   **Secure Disposal:** Properly destroying data when no longer needed

#### **6.3.2 Member Rights Management**

**Rights Request Processing:**

-   **Request Receipt:** Receiving and acknowledging rights requests

-   **Identity Verification:** Confirming the identity of requestors

-   **Information Retrieval:** Locating and compiling requested
    > information

-   **Response Preparation:** Preparing clear and comprehensive
    > responses

-   **Timely Delivery:** Providing responses within regulatory
    > timeframes

**Rights Implementation:**

-   **Access Rights:** Providing copies of personal data and processing
    > information

-   **Correction Rights:** Updating or correcting inaccurate information

-   **Deletion Rights:** Removing personal data when appropriate

-   **Portability Rights:** Providing data in machine-readable format

-   **Objection Rights:** Stopping processing when individuals object
