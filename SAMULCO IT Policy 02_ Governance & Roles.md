## **1. PURPOSE**

This policy establishes the IT governance framework for Sta. Ana
Multipurpose Cooperative (SAMULCO), defining the organizational
structure, roles, responsibilities, and decision-making processes for
effective IT management and oversight.

## **2. SCOPE**

This policy applies to all personnel involved in IT governance,
management, and operations within SAMULCO, including Board members,
management, IT staff, and end-users.

## **3. IT GOVERNANCE STRUCTURE**

### **3.1 Three Lines of Defense Model**

SAMULCO adopts a three lines of defense model for IT governance:

#### **3.1.1 First Line of Defense**

-   **Business Operations:** Day-to-day IT risk management

-   **IT Operations Team:** System administration and maintenance

-   **End Users:** Compliance with IT policies and procedures

#### **3.1.2 Second Line of Defense**

-   **IT Risk Management:** Oversight and monitoring

-   **Compliance Function:** Regulatory and policy compliance

-   **Information Security Office:** Security governance and oversight

#### **3.1.3 Third Line of Defense**

-   **Internal Audit:** Independent assurance and validation

-   **External Audit:** Third-party validation and compliance assessment

## **4. GOVERNANCE BODIES AND COMMITTEES**

### **4.1 Board of Directors**

#### **4.1.1 Composition**

-   Board Chairman

-   Board Members

-   Independent Directors (where applicable)

#### **4.1.2 IT-Related Responsibilities**

-   **Strategic Oversight:** Approve IT strategy and major investments

-   **Policy Approval:** Endorse IT policies and governance framework

-   **Risk Appetite:** Define acceptable levels of IT risk

-   **Resource Allocation:** Approve IT budgets and major expenditures

-   **Performance Monitoring:** Review IT performance metrics and
    > reports

-   **Regulatory Compliance:** Ensure adherence to applicable laws and
    > regulations

#### **4.1.3 Meeting Frequency**

-   Quarterly IT governance reviews

-   Ad-hoc meetings for critical IT issues

-   Annual IT strategy sessions

### **4.2 ICT Committee**

#### **4.2.1 Composition**

-   **Chairman:** Board Member with IT expertise or experience

-   **Members:**

    -   General Manager

    -   Chief ICT Officer

    -   Branch Manager (rotating membership)

    -   Risk Management Officer

    -   Compliance Officer

    -   External IT Advisor (optional)

#### **4.2.2 Responsibilities**

-   **Policy Development:** Recommend IT policies and procedures

-   **Investment Oversight:** Evaluate major IT investments and projects

-   **Risk Management:** Monitor IT risks and mitigation strategies

-   **Vendor Management:** Oversee major IT vendor relationships

-   **Innovation:** Guide digital transformation initiatives

-   **Performance Review:** Assess IT department performance

#### **4.2.3 Authority**

-   Recommend IT policies to the Board for approval

-   Approve IT projects within defined thresholds

-   Direct IT risk management activities

-   Authorize emergency IT expenditures

#### **4.2.4 Meeting Schedule**

-   Monthly regular meetings

-   Quarterly strategic reviews

-   Emergency meetings as required

### **4.3 IT Steering Committee**

#### **4.3.1 Composition**

-   **Chairman:** CEO

-   **Members:**

    -   Chief ICT Officer

    -   Department Heads

    -   Branch Managers

    -   Key System Users

#### **4.3.2 Responsibilities**

-   **Project Prioritization:** Prioritize IT projects and initiatives

-   **Change Management:** Oversee IT changes and implementations

-   **User Requirements:** Gather and validate business requirements

-   **Communication:** Facilitate IT-business alignment

-   **Training Coordination:** Plan user training and support programs

## **5. ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES**

### **5.1 Board of Directors**

#### **5.1.1 Chairman of the Board**

-   **Ultimate Accountability:** Overall responsibility for IT
    > governance

-   **Strategic Leadership:** Champion digital transformation
    > initiatives

-   **Stakeholder Communication:** Represent IT matters to external
    > stakeholders

-   **Crisis Management:** Lead response to major IT incidents or
    > breaches

#### **5.1.2 Board Members**

-   **Fiduciary Duty:** Exercise due diligence in IT oversight

-   **Policy Oversight:** Review and approve IT policies

-   **Risk Tolerance:** Define acceptable IT risk levels

-   **Performance Monitoring:** Monitor IT performance against
    > objectives

### **5.2 Management Team**

#### **5.2.1 General Manager**

-   **Executive Accountability:** Overall responsibility for IT
    > operations

-   **Strategic Implementation:** Execute Board-approved IT strategy

-   **Resource Management:** Allocate resources for IT operations

-   **Cross-functional Coordination:** Ensure IT-business alignment

-   **Regulatory Compliance:** Ensure compliance with applicable
    > regulations

-   **Incident Escalation:** Manage major IT incidents and crises

**Specific IT Responsibilities:**

-   Approve IT policies and procedures within authority limits

-   Monitor IT performance metrics and KPIs

-   Ensure adequate IT staffing and competencies

-   Coordinate with regulatory authorities on IT matters

-   Lead digital transformation initiatives

#### **5.2.2 Chief ICT Officer**

**Primary Responsibilities:**

-   **IT Strategy:** Develop and implement IT strategic plans

-   **Policy Implementation:** Ensure compliance with IT policies

-   **Team Management:** Lead and manage IT staff

-   **Vendor Management:** Manage IT vendor relationships

-   **Budget Management:** Manage IT budget and expenditures

-   **Risk Management:** Identify and mitigate IT risks

**Operational Duties:**

-   Plan and coordinate IT projects

-   Oversee system implementations and upgrades

-   Ensure business continuity and disaster recovery preparedness

-   Monitor system performance and availability

-   Coordinate with external auditors and regulators

-   Report IT metrics to management and Board

**Technical Leadership:**

-   Define technical standards and architecture

-   Approve technology choices and solutions

-   Oversee security implementation and monitoring

-   Ensure compliance with technical policies

-   Lead technical innovation and improvements

#### **5.2.3 Department Heads/Branch Managers**

-   **Business Requirements:** Define IT requirements for their areas

-   **User Management:** Manage IT access for their staff

-   **Policy Compliance:** Ensure staff compliance with IT policies

-   **Risk Identification:** Identify IT risks in their operations

-   **Training Coordination:** Facilitate IT training for their teams

-   **Incident Reporting:** Report IT incidents and issues promptly

### **5.3 IT Team Structure and Roles**

#### **5.3.1 Chief ICT Officer**

As defined in Section 5.2.2 above.

#### **5.3.2 IT Specialist in Documentation (ISD)**

-   **Policy Enforcement:** Monitor and enforce IT policies

-   **Documentation:** Maintain IT policies and procedures

-   **Compliance Monitoring:** Ensure regulatory compliance

-   **Audit Coordination:** Coordinate with internal and external
    > auditors

-   **Risk Assessment:** Conduct IT risk assessments

-   **Training Materials:** Develop IT training materials

#### **5.3.3 IT Specialist in Technical (IST)**

-   **System Administration:** Manage servers, networks, and systems

-   **Technical Support:** Provide first and second level support

-   **Security Implementation:** Implement security measures and
    > controls

-   **Maintenance:** Perform system maintenance and updates

-   **Installation:** Install and configure hardware and software

-   **Troubleshooting:** Resolve technical issues and problems

#### **5.3.4 System Developer**

-   **Application Development:** Develop in-house software applications

-   **System Maintenance:** Debug and update existing systems

-   **Integration:** Integrate systems and applications

-   **Testing:** Perform system and user acceptance testing

-   **Documentation:** Document system specifications and procedures

-   **Support:** Provide application support and training

#### **5.3.5 Database Administrator (if applicable)**

-   **Database Management:** Administer database systems

-   **Performance Tuning:** Optimize database performance

-   **Backup and Recovery:** Ensure data backup and recovery procedures

-   **Security:** Implement database security measures

-   **Monitoring:** Monitor database performance and availability

-   **Maintenance:** Perform database maintenance activities

### **5.4 Employee Responsibilities**

#### **5.4.1 All Employees**

-   **Policy Compliance:** Adhere to all IT policies and procedures

-   **Security Awareness:** Maintain awareness of security threats and
    > best practices

-   **Incident Reporting:** Report IT incidents and security concerns
    > promptly

-   **Training Participation:** Participate in mandatory IT training
    > programs

-   **Resource Protection:** Protect IT resources from misuse or damage

-   **Continuous Learning:** Stay updated on relevant IT developments

**Specific Obligations:**

-   Use strong passwords and protect authentication credentials

-   Report suspicious activities or security incidents immediately

-   Comply with data handling and privacy requirements

-   Use IT resources only for authorized business purposes

-   Participate in security awareness programs

-   Follow change management procedures

#### **5.4.2 System Users**

-   **Proper Usage:** Use systems according to established procedures

-   **Data Quality:** Maintain data accuracy and integrity

-   **Security Compliance:** Follow security protocols and procedures

-   **Change Management:** Follow proper procedures for system changes

-   **Documentation:** Document work procedures and system usage

-   **Feedback:** Provide feedback on system performance and usability

### **5.5 Third-Party Vendor Responsibilities**

#### **5.5.1 IT Service Providers**

-   **Service Delivery:** Deliver services according to agreed SLAs

-   **Security Compliance:** Comply with SAMULCO security requirements

-   **Incident Response:** Respond to incidents within agreed timeframes

-   **Reporting:** Provide regular service reports and metrics

-   **Documentation:** Maintain proper documentation of services

-   **Continuous Improvement:** Identify and implement service
    > improvements

#### **5.5.2 Software/Hardware Vendors**

-   **Product Support:** Provide technical support and maintenance

-   **Security Updates:** Deliver security patches and updates promptly

-   **Compliance:** Ensure products comply with relevant standards

-   **Training:** Provide user and administrator training

-   **Documentation:** Maintain comprehensive product documentation

-   **Lifecycle Management:** Provide product lifecycle guidance

### **6.2 Escalation Procedures**

#### **6.2.1 Operational Issues**

1.  **Level 1:** IT Specialist resolves routine issues

2.  **Level 2:** Chief ICT Officer addresses complex technical issues

3.  **Level 3:** CEO handles business-critical issues

4.  **Level 4:** ICT Committee addresses strategic issues

5.  **Level 5:** Board of Directors handles crisis situations

#### **6.2.2 Security Incidents**

1.  **Minor:** IT Specialist handles and reports

2.  **Major:** Chief ICT Officer leads response and escalates

3.  **Critical:** CEO leads crisis response

4.  **Catastrophic:** Board Chairman leads overall response

## **7. REPORTING AND COMMUNICATION**

### **7.1 Regular Reporting**

#### **7.1.1 Monthly Reports**

-   **Chief ICT Officer to CEO:** Operational metrics and issues

-   **IT Team:** Individual accomplishment reports

-   **Department Heads:** IT-related issues and requirements

#### **7.1.2 Quarterly Reports**

-   **General Manager to Board:** IT performance dashboard

-   **ICT Committee:** Strategic progress and risk assessment

-   **Compliance Reports:** Regulatory and policy compliance status

#### **7.1.3 Annual Reports**

-   **IT Strategy Review:** Assessment of strategic objectives

-   **Risk Assessment:** Comprehensive IT risk evaluation

-   **Budget Review:** IT expenditure analysis and planning

### **7.2 Communication Channels**

#### **7.2.1 Formal Channels**

-   Board meetings and resolutions

-   Committee meetings and minutes

-   Written reports and memoranda

-   Official email communications

#### **7.2.2 Informal Channels**

-   Regular one-on-one meetings

-   Team meetings and briefings

-   Cross-functional collaboration

-   Stakeholder consultations

## **8. PERFORMANCE MEASUREMENT**

### **8.1 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)**

#### **8.1.1 Governance KPIs**

-   Policy compliance rate (Target: 100%)

-   Training completion rate (Target: 95%)

-   Audit finding resolution time (Target: \<30 days)

-   Board/Committee meeting attendance (Target: \>80%)

#### **8.1.2 Operational KPIs**

-   System availability (Target: 99.5%)

-   Incident resolution time (Target: \<4 hours for critical)

-   User satisfaction score (Target: \>4.0/5.0)

-   Budget variance (Target: \<5%)

-   Identification of improvement opportunities

-   Development of enhancement roadmap

### **9.2 Feedback Mechanisms**

-   Regular stakeholder feedback sessions

-   Employee suggestion programs

-   Customer/member feedback integration

-   Vendor performance reviews
