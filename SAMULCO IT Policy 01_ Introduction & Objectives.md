## **1. INTRODUCTION**

Sta. Ana Multipurpose Cooperative (SAMULCO) recognizes that Information
Technology (IT) is fundamental to achieving our cooperative\'s mission
of providing quality financial and business services to our members.
This comprehensive IT Policy framework establishes the governance,
standards, and procedures necessary to ensure the secure, reliable, and
efficient use of IT resources while protecting member data and
supporting cooperative operations.

## **2. PURPOSE AND OBJECTIVES**

### **2.1 Primary Purpose**

This IT Policy serves to:

-   **Support Cooperative Operations:** Ensure IT systems and services
    > enable efficient delivery of cooperative services to members and
    > stakeholders

-   **Protect Member Data:** Implement robust security measures to
    > safeguard member information and maintain confidentiality

-   **Ensure Compliance:** Maintain adherence to applicable laws,
    > regulations, and industry standards

-   **Risk Management:** Identify, assess, and mitigate IT-related risks
    > to the cooperative

-   **Business Continuity:** Ensure critical systems remain available
    > and recoverable in case of disruptions

### **2.2 Specific Objectives**

#### **2.2.1 Operational Excellence**

-   Establish standardized IT processes and procedures

-   Ensure optimal performance and reliability of IT systems

-   Support digital transformation initiatives

-   Enable efficient resource utilization

#### **2.2.2 Security and Privacy**

-   Protect sensitive member and cooperative data

-   Implement defense-in-depth security measures

-   Ensure privacy compliance and data protection

-   Maintain system integrity and availability

#### **2.2.3 Regulatory Compliance**

-   Comply with Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173)

-   Adhere to Cooperative Development Authority (CDA) requirements

-   Meet Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) guidelines where applicable

-   Align with international standards (ISO 27001, COBIT)

#### **2.2.4 Governance and Risk Management**

-   Establish clear IT governance structure and accountability

-   Implement comprehensive risk management framework

-   Ensure effective oversight and monitoring

-   Maintain audit trail and compliance reporting

## **3. SCOPE OF APPLICATION**

### **3.1 Personnel Coverage**

This IT Policy applies to all individuals who interact with SAMULCO\'s
IT resources, including but not limited to:

#### **3.1.1 Internal Personnel**

-   **Board of Directors:** Governance oversight and policy approval

-   **Management Team:** Strategic direction and resource allocation

-   **Employees:** Daily operations and compliance adherence

-   **Officers:** Leadership and supervisory responsibilities

-   **Temporary Staff:** Project-based and seasonal workers

-   **Interns and Trainees:** Learning and development participants

#### **3.1.2 External Parties**

-   **Contractors:** Service providers and consultants

-   **Third-party Service Providers:** Outsourced IT services and
    > vendors

-   **Business Partners:** Collaborative service providers

-   **Auditors:** Internal and external audit personnel

-   **Visitors:** Authorized guests with limited IT access

### **3.2 Technology Coverage**

This policy encompasses all IT resources owned, operated, or used by
SAMULCO:

#### **3.2.1 Hardware Assets**

-   Servers and data center equipment

-   Desktop computers and laptops

-   Mobile devices and tablets

-   Network infrastructure equipment

-   Printers and peripheral devices

-   Storage devices and backup systems

#### **3.2.2 Software Assets**

-   Operating systems and applications

-   Database management systems

-   Cooperative Accounting System (IACCS2013)

-   Cloud-based applications and services

-   Security software and tools

-   Licensed and open-source software

#### **3.2.3 Network and Communication**

-   Local Area Networks (LAN) and Wide Area Networks (WAN)

-   Internet and intranet services

-   Email and messaging systems

-   Voice over IP (VoIP) systems

-   Wireless networks and access points

-   Remote access and VPN services

#### **3.2.4 Data and Information**

-   Member information and records

-   Financial data and transactions

-   Operational and administrative data

-   Backup and archived information

-   Intellectual property and confidential data

## **4. ALIGNMENT WITH COOPERATIVE VALUES**

### **4.1 Cooperative Principles Integration**

Our IT policies reflect the cooperative principles:

-   **Member Economic Participation:** IT systems support transparent
    > financial services

-   **Democratic Member Control:** Technology enables member
    > participation and engagement

-   **Autonomy and Independence:** IT governance ensures cooperative
    > self-reliance

-   **Education and Training:** Technology facilitates continuous
    > learning and development

-   **Cooperation Among Cooperatives:** IT systems support
    > inter-cooperative collaboration

-   **Concern for Community:** Technology initiatives benefit the
    > broader community

### **4.2 Ethical Technology Use**

SAMULCO is committed to:

-   Responsible and ethical use of technology

-   Transparency in IT operations and decision-making

-   Sustainability in IT resource utilization

-   Social responsibility in technology adoption

## **7. SUCCESS METRICS**

SAMULCO will measure the effectiveness of this IT Policy through:

### **7.1 Security Metrics**

-   Zero tolerance for data breaches involving member information

-   99.9% system availability for critical applications

-   100% compliance with security awareness training

-   Regular security assessments with satisfactory ratings

### **7.2 Operational Metrics**

-   Reduced IT-related incidents and downtime

-   Improved user satisfaction scores

-   Efficient incident response and resolution times

-   Cost-effective IT resource utilization

### **7.3 Compliance Metrics**

-   100% compliance with applicable regulations

-   Successful completion of internal and external audits

-   Timely implementation of regulatory updates

-   Effective risk management and mitigation
