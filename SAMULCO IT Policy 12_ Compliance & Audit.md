## **1. PURPOSE**

This policy establishes the framework for ensuring compliance with
applicable laws, regulations, and standards governing Information
Technology operations at Sta. Ana Multipurpose Cooperative (SAMULCO). It
defines requirements for regulatory compliance, internal and external
audits, risk assessments, and continuous monitoring to maintain
regulatory adherence and operational excellence.

## **2. SCOPE**

This policy applies to all:

-   IT systems, applications, and infrastructure

-   Data processing and information management activities

-   IT governance and risk management processes

-   Personnel involved in IT operations and management

-   Third-party service providers and vendors

-   Regulatory reporting and compliance activities

-   Internal and external audit processes
