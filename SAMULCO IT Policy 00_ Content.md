# **SAMULCO IT Policy 00: Content**

## **1. Introduction & Objectives**

-   Purpose of the IT policy (support cooperative operations, protect
    > member data, ensure compliance).

-   Scope (applies to staff, officers, contractors, third-party
    > providers).

-   Alignment with cooperative values, regulatory compliance, and
    > industry standards (e.g., ISO 27001, BSP guidelines).

## **2. Governance & Roles**

-   **IT Governance Structure**: ICT Committee, IT Department, Audit &
    > Compliance.

-   **Roles & Responsibilities**:

    -   Board / Management: policy approval, oversight.

    -   IT Team: implementation, monitoring, incident handling.

    -   Employees: adherence, reporting incidents.

    -   Third-party vendors: compliance with cooperative standards.

## **3. Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)**

-   Rules for using cooperative-owned devices, email, internet, cloud
    > services.

-   Prohibition of personal, illegal, or non-coop related activities.

-   Social media and communication guidelines.

## **4. Data Management & Privacy**

-   Data classification (public, internal, confidential, highly
    > confidential).

-   Member information handling and storage.

-   Data retention, archival, and destruction policies.

-   Compliance with **Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173)**.

## **5. Information Security**

-   **Password Policy** (complexity, expiration, MFA).

-   **Access Control** (role-based, least privilege principle).

-   Device and workstation security (auto-lock, antivirus, patching).

-   Encryption of sensitive data at rest and in transit.

-   Remote work security (VPN, approved devices).

## **6. Systems & Network Security**

-   Firewall, IDS/IPS, endpoint security requirements.

-   Patch management and updates.

-   Email security (anti-phishing, spam filters).

-   Use of secured cooperative Wi-Fi (guest vs staff networks).

## **7. Core Banking System & Financial Applications**

-   User access segregation (tellers, loans, admin).

-   Audit logs for all transactions.

-   Change management process for updates/upgrades.

-   Vendor support agreements and SLAs.

## **8. Business Continuity & Disaster Recovery**

-   Backup policy (frequency, storage locations, testing restores).

-   Disaster recovery plan (RTO, RPO targets).

-   Business continuity planning (BCP drills, alternative sites).

## **9. Incident Management**

-   Definition of security incident (breach, malware, unauthorized
    > access).

-   Incident reporting procedures (who to notify, timeframe).

-   Escalation levels (minor, major, critical).

-   Root cause analysis and corrective action process.

## **10. IT Asset Management**

-   Inventory of hardware, software, licenses.

-   Procurement guidelines (approved vendors, compatibility).

-   Asset disposal policy (data wiping, environmentally safe disposal).

## **11. Third-Party & Cloud Services**

-   Due diligence requirements (vendor security, financial stability).

-   Data processing agreements (DPA compliance).

-   Outsourced IT services and SLA monitoring.

-   Restrictions on storing member data on unauthorized cloud apps.

## **12. Compliance & Audit**

-   Alignment with BSP regulations (if cooperative is under BSP
    > supervision).

-   Compliance with **Cooperative Development Authority (CDA)**
    > IT-related policies.

-   Internal and external audit requirements.

-   Regular IT risk assessments.

## **13. Training & Awareness**

-   Regular staff training on cybersecurity, phishing, data privacy.

-   New employee IT onboarding program.

-   Periodic policy review and refresher sessions.

## **14. Policy Enforcement**

-   Disciplinary actions for violations.

-   Reporting mechanism for suspected policy breaches.

-   Continuous improvement and updates to policy.

## **15. Review & Updates**

-   Frequency of review (annually or bi-annually).

-   Approval process for updates (Board/ICT Committee).

## **16. Change Management**

-   Framework for managing changes to ICT systems and projects.

-   Roles of Change Advisory Board (CAB) and Implementation Team.

-   Process for categorization, approval, testing, and deployment.
