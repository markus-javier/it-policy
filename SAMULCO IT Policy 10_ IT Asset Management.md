## **1. PURPOSE**

This policy establishes the framework for comprehensive management of
Information Technology (IT) assets at Sta. Ana Multipurpose Cooperative
(SAMULCO). It defines procedures for asset identification, procurement,
inventory management, lifecycle tracking, maintenance, and secure
disposal to ensure optimal value, security, and compliance throughout
the asset lifecycle.

## **2. SCOPE**

This policy applies to all:

-   IT hardware assets (servers, workstations, network equipment, mobile
    > devices)

-   Software assets (applications, operating systems, licenses)

-   Digital assets (data, intellectual property, digital certificates)

-   Cloud services and subscription-based assets

-   IT-related contracts and service agreements

-   Personnel responsible for IT asset management

-   Third-party vendors and service providers

### **3.2 Asset Criticality Classification**

#### **3.2.1 Critical Assets**

**Definition:** Assets essential for core business operations whose
failure would cause immediate business disruption.

**Examples:**

-   Core Banking System servers and databases

-   Primary network infrastructure and connectivity

-   Financial transaction processing systems

-   Member-facing applications and services

-   Primary data storage and backup systems

**Management Requirements:**

-   24/7 monitoring and support

-   Redundant configurations and failover capabilities

-   Quarterly maintenance and health checks

-   Priority access to vendor support and resources

-   Executive approval for significant changes or disposal

#### **3.2.2 Important Assets**

**Definition:** Assets that support significant business functions whose
failure would cause measurable business impact.

**Examples:**

-   Email and communication systems

-   Document management and collaboration platforms

-   Branch office equipment and local servers

-   Reporting and analytics systems

-   Administrative and HR systems

**Management Requirements:**

-   Business hours monitoring and support

-   Regular maintenance and health monitoring

-   Semi-annual reviews and assessments

-   Standard vendor support and maintenance contracts

-   Management approval for changes or disposal

#### **3.2.3 Standard Assets**

**Definition:** Assets that support routine business activities whose
failure would have limited business impact.

**Examples:**

-   Individual workstations and laptops

-   Standard office printers and peripherals

-   Development and testing equipment

-   Training and presentation systems

-   Personal productivity software

**Management Requirements:**

-   Basic monitoring and user support

-   Routine maintenance and updates

-   Annual reviews and lifecycle assessment

-   Standard procurement and replacement procedures

-   Department head approval for disposal

#### **3.2.4 Low-Impact Assets**

**Definition:** Assets with minimal business impact whose failure would
not significantly affect operations.

**Examples:**

-   Spare and replacement hardware

-   Personal mobile devices and accessories

-   Legacy systems and equipment

-   Non-business applications and tools

-   Demonstration and marketing equipment

**Management Requirements:**

-   Minimal monitoring and support

-   Basic security and compliance controls

-   Periodic inventory and status verification

-   Simplified disposal and replacement procedures

-   User or supervisor approval for disposal

## **4. ASSET INVENTORY MANAGEMENT**

### **4.1 Asset Discovery and Registration**

#### **4.1.1 Automated Discovery**

**Network Scanning and Discovery:**

-   Regular automated network scans for connected devices

-   Asset discovery tools for hardware and software identification

-   Network mapping and topology documentation

-   MAC address and IP address tracking

-   Service and application discovery and cataloging

**Agent-Based Discovery:**

-   Endpoint agents for detailed hardware and software inventory

-   Automated reporting of system configurations and changes

-   Real-time asset status and health monitoring

-   Software license usage and compliance tracking

-   Security posture and patch status reporting

#### **4.1.2 Manual Registration**

**Asset Registration Process:**

1.  **Initial Assessment:** Identify asset type, criticality, and
    > business purpose

2.  **Documentation:** Complete asset registration form with required
    > details

3.  **Tagging:** Apply physical asset tags with unique identifiers

4.  **Database Entry:** Enter asset information into centralized
    > inventory system

5.  **Assignment:** Link asset to responsible user, department, and
    > location

6.  **Verification:** Validate asset information and approve
    > registration

7.  **Monitoring:** Begin lifecycle tracking and management procedures

**Registration Requirements:**

-   **Asset Information:** Model, serial number, specifications, and
    > configuration

-   **Financial Information:** Purchase cost, depreciation schedule, and
    > warranty details

-   **Ownership Information:** Assigned user, department, and budget
    > responsibility

-   **Location Information:** Physical location, network address, and
    > access requirements

-   **Support Information:** Vendor contacts, support contracts, and
    > maintenance schedules

### **4.2 Inventory Database Management**

#### **4.2.1 Asset Database Structure**

**Core Asset Information:**

-   **Identification:** Unique asset ID, tag number, and barcode

-   **Classification:** Asset type, category, and criticality level

-   **Specifications:** Technical details, capacity, and performance
    > characteristics

-   **Financial:** Purchase price, depreciation, current value, and
    > total cost of ownership

-   **Operational:** Status, condition, utilization, and performance
    > metrics

**Relationship Management:**

-   **Dependencies:** Related assets and system interdependencies

-   **Configurations:** Hardware and software configuration
    > relationships

-   **Assignments:** User and departmental assignments and
    > responsibilities

-   **Locations:** Physical and logical location tracking and history

-   **Contracts:** Vendor relationships, support agreements, and
    > warranties

#### **4.2.2 Database Maintenance**

**Regular Updates:**

-   **Daily:** Automated status updates and change detection

-   **Weekly:** Asset assignment and location verification

-   **Monthly:** Configuration change validation and approval

-   **Quarterly:** Comprehensive inventory reconciliation and audit

-   **Annually:** Full physical inventory and database validation

**Data Quality Management:**

-   Standardized data formats and validation rules

-   Automated data quality checks and error detection

-   Exception reporting and resolution procedures

-   Regular data cleanup and normalization

-   Integration with other business systems and databases

### **4.3 Physical Asset Tracking**

#### **4.3.1 Asset Tagging System**

**Tagging Standards:**

-   **Physical Tags:** Durable, tamper-evident labels with unique
    > identifiers

-   **Barcode System:** Machine-readable codes for automated tracking

-   **QR Codes:** Quick response codes for mobile device scanning

-   **RFID Tags:** Radio frequency identification for automated tracking

-   **Engraving:** Permanent marking on high-value or critical assets

**Tag Management:**

-   Standardized tagging procedures and requirements

-   Tag placement guidelines for different asset types

-   Tag replacement and maintenance procedures

-   Lost or damaged tag reporting and replacement

-   Integration with inventory database and tracking systems

#### **4.3.2 Location Management**

**Location Tracking:**

-   **Primary Location:** Assigned department, office, or facility

-   **Sub-Location:** Specific room, desk, or rack location

-   **Temporary Location:** Loan, repair, or temporary assignment
    > location

-   **Geographic Coordinates:** GPS coordinates for mobile or outdoor
    > assets

-   **Network Location:** IP address, VLAN, and network segment
    > information

**Movement Tracking:**

-   Asset movement authorization and approval procedures

-   Check-in and check-out procedures for temporary assignments

-   Location change notification and database updates

-   Asset transfer between departments or locations

-   Audit trail of location changes and movement history

3.  **RFQ/RFP Development:** Create detailed request for quotation or
    > proposal

5.  **Contract Negotiation:** Negotiate terms, pricing, and service
    > levels

    > authorizations

## **8. ASSET DISPOSAL POLICY**

#### **8.1.1 Asset Retirement Criteria**

**Technical Criteria:**

-   End of vendor support and maintenance availability

-   Hardware failure rate exceeding acceptable thresholds

-   Performance degradation affecting business operations

-   Security vulnerabilities with no available remediation

-   Incompatibility with current systems and requirements

**Financial Criteria:**

-   High maintenance costs relative to replacement costs

-   Low residual value and depreciation completion

-   Opportunity cost of continued operation

-   Budget availability for replacement asset

-   Total cost of ownership optimization

**Authorization Requirements:**

### **8.2 Data Sanitization and Security**

#### **8.2.1 Data Classification and Handling**

**Data Sensitivity Levels:**

-   **Public:** No special handling requirements for disposal

-   **Internal:** Standard data wiping and disposal procedures

-   **Confidential:** Enhanced data destruction and verification

-   **Highly Confidential:** Physical destruction and certified disposal

**Sanitization Methods:**

-   **Software Wiping:** DoD 5220.22-M standard overwriting

-   **Degaussing:** Magnetic field destruction of magnetic media

-   **Physical Destruction:** Shredding, crushing, or incineration

-   **Cryptographic Erasure:** Encryption key destruction for encrypted
    > data

-   **Professional Services:** Certified third-party destruction
    > services

#### **8.2.2 Sanitization Verification**

**Verification Procedures:**

-   Pre-sanitization data inventory and classification

-   Sanitization process monitoring and documentation

-   Post-sanitization verification and testing

-   Certificate of destruction or sanitization

-   Audit trail and compliance documentation

**Quality Assurance:**

-   Independent verification of sanitization completeness

-   Random sampling and testing procedures

-   Vendor certification and compliance validation

-   Exception handling and re-processing procedures

-   Continuous improvement and process enhancement
