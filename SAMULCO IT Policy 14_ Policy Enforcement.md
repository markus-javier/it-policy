## **1. PURPOSE**

This policy establishes the framework for enforcing IT policies and
procedures at Sta. Ana Multipurpose Cooperative (SAMULCO). It defines
compliance monitoring mechanisms, violation reporting procedures,
disciplinary actions, and continuous improvement processes to ensure
consistent adherence to IT policies and maintain a strong security
culture.

## **2. SCOPE**

This policy applies to all:

-   SAMULCO employees (permanent, temporary, contractual)

-   Board members and officers

-   Third-party contractors and consultants

-   Vendors with access to SAMULCO systems

-   Volunteers and interns

-   All IT policies and procedures established by SAMULCO

## **3. POLICY ENFORCEMENT FRAMEWORK**

### **3.1 Enforcement Principles**

#### **3.1.1 Core Enforcement Principles**

**Fair and Consistent Application:**

-   Equal treatment of all individuals regardless of position or status

-   Consistent application of policies across all departments and
    > locations

-   Transparent enforcement procedures and decision-making processes

-   Clear communication of expectations and consequences

-   Regular review and updates to maintain fairness and relevance

**Progressive Discipline:**

-   Graduated response system based on violation severity and frequency

-   Educational approach for first-time or minor violations

-   Escalating consequences for repeat or serious violations

-   Opportunity for improvement and remediation

-   Balancing corrective action with business continuity needs

**Due Process:**

-   Fair and thorough investigation of alleged violations

-   Right to respond and provide explanation or defense

-   Appropriate representation and support during proceedings

-   Appeals process for disputed enforcement actions

-   Documentation and record-keeping of all enforcement activities

#### **3.1.2 Enforcement Objectives**

**Primary Objectives:**

-   Ensure consistent compliance with IT policies and procedures

-   Maintain security posture and protect organizational assets

-   Foster a culture of accountability and responsibility

-   Deter future policy violations through appropriate consequences

-   Support continuous improvement and policy effectiveness

**Behavioral Objectives:**

-   Encourage proactive compliance and positive security behaviors

-   Promote reporting of security concerns and potential violations

-   Support learning and development from policy violations

-   Build trust and confidence in policy enforcement fairness

-   Strengthen organizational security culture and awareness

### **3.2 Governance Structure**

## **4. POLICY VIOLATION CLASSIFICATION**

### **4.1 Violation Severity Levels**

#### **4.1.1 Critical Violations (Level 1)**

**Characteristics:**

-   Immediate threat to organizational security or business operations

-   Intentional violation of security policies with malicious intent

-   Significant compromise of member data or financial information

-   Criminal activity or behavior subject to legal prosecution

-   Regulatory compliance violations with potential penalties

**Examples:**

-   Intentional unauthorized access to confidential member data

-   Deliberate installation of malware or malicious software

-   Theft or misuse of organizational IT assets

-   Intentional disclosure of confidential information to unauthorized
    > parties

-   Deliberate circumvention of security controls for personal gain

**Immediate Actions:**

-   Immediate suspension of system access and privileges

-   Isolation of affected systems and containment of damage

-   Law enforcement notification if criminal activity suspected

-   Legal counsel consultation for potential legal action

-   Regulatory notification if required by law or regulation

#### **4.1.2 High Violations (Level 2)**

**Characteristics:**

-   Serious policy violation with significant security or business
    > impact

-   Reckless or grossly negligent behavior causing security risks

-   Repeated violations of the same or different policies

-   Violation affecting multiple systems or users

-   Failure to report known security incidents or violations

**Examples:**

-   Sharing passwords or authentication credentials with unauthorized
    > persons

-   Unauthorized installation of software or system modifications

-   Accessing systems or data beyond authorized scope

-   Failure to follow security procedures resulting in security incident

-   Negligent handling of confidential information causing exposure risk

**Standard Actions:**

-   Temporary suspension of system access pending investigation

-   Formal written warning and performance improvement plan

-   Mandatory retraining and certification requirements

-   Increased supervision and monitoring of activities

-   Potential progressive discipline up to termination

#### **4.1.3 Medium Violations (Level 3)**

**Characteristics:**

-   Policy violation with moderate security or business impact

-   Failure to follow established procedures or guidelines

-   Negligent behavior that creates security vulnerabilities

-   First-time violation of important security policies

-   Non-compliance with training or awareness requirements

**Examples:**

-   Failure to report suspicious activities or potential security
    > incidents

-   Inappropriate use of internet or email systems

-   Failure to secure workstation or mobile devices properly

-   Non-compliance with software licensing requirements

-   Violation of clean desk or physical security policies

**Standard Actions:**

-   Verbal warning and counseling session

-   Mandatory retraining on relevant policies and procedures

-   Written documentation of violation and corrective action

-   Short-term increased supervision and monitoring

-   Performance improvement planning if appropriate

#### **4.1.4 Low Violations (Level 4)**

**Characteristics:**

-   Minor policy violation with limited security or business impact

-   Inadvertent or accidental violation due to lack of knowledge

-   First-time violation of administrative or procedural policies

-   Violation that does not compromise security or operations

-   Non-compliance with minor administrative requirements

**Examples:**

-   Failure to complete non-critical training within specified
    > timeframes

-   Minor violations of acceptable use policies

-   Inadvertent policy violations due to lack of awareness

-   Administrative non-compliance with documentation requirements

-   Minor physical security or facility access violations

**Standard Actions:**

-   Informal counseling and education about policy requirements

-   Additional training or awareness materials provision

-   Documentation of violation for tracking and trend analysis

-   Corrective action to prevent future similar violations

-   No formal disciplinary action unless pattern develops

### **4.2 Aggravating and Mitigating Factors**

#### **4.2.1 Aggravating Factors**

**Factors Increasing Severity:**

-   **Intent:** Deliberate and intentional violation of known policies

-   **Impact:** Significant damage to systems, data, or business
    > operations

-   **Scope:** Violation affecting multiple systems, users, or
    > departments

-   **Frequency:** Repeated violations or pattern of non-compliance

-   **Cooperation:** Failure to cooperate with investigation or
    > remediation efforts

-   **Concealment:** Attempts to hide, cover up, or destroy evidence of
    > violations

-   **Position:** Abuse of trusted position or privileged access

-   **Timing:** Violations during critical business periods or emergency
    > situations

#### **4.2.2 Mitigating Factors**

**Factors Reducing Severity:**

-   **Self-Reporting:** Voluntary disclosure of violation before
    > discovery

-   **Cooperation:** Full cooperation with investigation and remediation
    > efforts

-   **Remediation:** Prompt action to correct violation and prevent
    > recurrence

-   **Intent:** Accidental or inadvertent violation without malicious
    > intent

-   **Training:** Lack of adequate training or awareness of policy
    > requirements

-   **First Offense:** First-time violation with no previous compliance
    > issues

-   **Impact:** Minimal actual or potential impact from the violation

-   **Circumstances:** Extraordinary circumstances or emergency
    > situations

### **4.3 Violation Documentation**

#### **4.3.1 Investigation Documentation**

**Initial Documentation:**

-   Date, time, and circumstances of violation discovery

-   Personnel involved or affected by the violation

-   Systems, data, or processes impacted by the violation

#### **4.3.2 Decision Documentation**

**Enforcement Decision Records:**

-   **Violation Classification:** Final determination of violation level
    > and category

-   **Disciplinary Action:** Specific disciplinary measures imposed

-   **Rationale:** Explanation of decision-making process and factors
    > considered

-   **Approval Authority:** Documentation of appropriate approval and
    > authorization

-   **Appeal Rights:** Information about appeals process and rights

**Implementation Documentation:**

-   **Action Implementation:** Records of disciplinary action
    > implementation

-   **Employee Communication:** Documentation of communication with
    > affected employee

    > requirements

    > follow-up actions

-   **Completion Verification:** Confirmation of successful action
    > completion

## **5. DISCIPLINARY ACTIONS**

### **5.1 Progressive Discipline System**

#### **5.1.1 Discipline Levels**

**Level 1 - Verbal Warning:**

-   **Application:** First-time minor violations or inadvertent policy
    > breaches

-   **Process:** Informal discussion with employee and immediate
    > supervisor

-   **Documentation:** Brief written record for supervisory files

-   **Follow-up:** Monitoring to ensure no repeat violations

-   **Duration:** Generally not retained in permanent personnel record

**Level 2 - Written Warning:**

-   **Application:** Repeat minor violations or first-time moderate
    > violations

-   **Process:** Formal written warning with specific improvement
    > expectations

-   **Documentation:** Written warning placed in employee personnel file

-   **Follow-up:** Regular monitoring and check-ins with supervisor

-   **Duration:** Typically remains active for 12 months

**Level 3 - Probationary Period:**

-   **Application:** Serious violations or repeated moderate violations

-   **Process:** Formal probationary period with performance improvement
    > plan

-   **Documentation:** Detailed performance improvement plan and
    > expectations

-   **Follow-up:** Weekly monitoring and progress reviews

-   **Duration:** Typically 90-180 days depending on violation severity

**Level 4 - Suspension:**

-   **Application:** Serious violations requiring immediate action and
    > investigation

-   **Process:** Temporary suspension with or without pay pending
    > investigation

-   **Documentation:** Formal suspension notice with terms and
    > conditions

-   **Follow-up:** Completion of investigation and final disposition

-   **Duration:** Variable based on investigation complexity and
    > findings

**Level 5 - Termination:**

-   **Application:** Critical violations, repeated serious violations,
    > or failed probation

-   **Process:** Formal termination following due process and
    > investigation

-   **Documentation:** Comprehensive termination documentation and
    > justification

-   **Follow-up:** Exit procedures and final administrative actions

-   **Duration:** Permanent separation from employment

#### **5.1.2 Alternative Disciplinary Actions**

**Retraining and Education:**

-   Mandatory additional training on relevant policies and procedures

-   Certification requirements for continued system access

-   Mentoring or coaching arrangements for skill development

-   Regular assessments to verify understanding and compliance

-   Documentation of training completion and competency validation

**Privilege Restriction:**

-   Temporary or permanent restriction of system access privileges

-   Supervised access requirements for sensitive systems or data

-   Limitation of administrative or advanced user privileges

-   Requirement for dual authorization or approval for certain
    > activities

-   Regular review and potential restoration of privileges

**Job Reassignment:**

-   Transfer to position with reduced security responsibilities

-   Assignment to role with limited access to sensitive information

-   Temporary or permanent change in job duties and responsibilities

-   Additional supervision and oversight requirements

-   Career development planning and alternative advancement paths

### **5.2 Immediate Action Procedures**

#### **5.2.1 Emergency Response Actions**

**Immediate Access Suspension:**

-   **Triggers:** Critical violations posing immediate security or
    > business risk

-   **Process:** Automatic or manual suspension of all system access

-   **Authority:** MIS Manager or designated security officer

-   **Duration:** Until investigation completion and management decision

-   **Communication:** Immediate notification to employee and supervisor

**System Isolation:**

-   **Triggers:** Suspected malicious activity or system compromise

-   **Process:** Isolation of affected systems from network and
    > resources

-   **Authority:** ICT Manager or Senior System Administrator

-   **Duration:** Until security investigation completion and system
    > remediation

-   **Communication:** Notification to affected users and stakeholders

#### **5.2.2 Investigation Procedures**

**Immediate Investigation:**

-   **Initiation:** Within 2 hours of critical violation discovery

-   **Team Assembly:** Investigation team including MIS, HR, and
    > management

-   **Evidence Preservation:** Immediate preservation of logs, data, and
    > evidence

-   **Interviews:** Prompt interviews with involved parties and
    > witnesses

-   **Reporting:** Initial findings report within 24 hours

**Formal Investigation:**

-   **Planning:** Comprehensive investigation plan and resource
    > allocation

-   **Evidence Collection:** Systematic collection and analysis of all
    > evidence

-   **Expert Consultation:** External experts or law enforcement as
    > needed

-   **Documentation:** Detailed investigation report and recommendations

-   **Decision:** Final determination and disciplinary action
    > recommendation

### **5.3 Appeals Process**

#### **5.3.1 Appeals Rights and Procedures**

**Appeal Rights:**

-   Right to appeal all formal disciplinary actions

-   Timely notification of appeal rights and procedures

-   Right to representation during appeals process

-   Right to present additional evidence or information

-   Right to independent review of disciplinary decision

**Appeal Process:**

1.  **Appeal Filing:** Written appeal within 10 business days of
    > disciplinary action

2.  **Initial Review:** HR and management review of appeal and
    > supporting documentation

3.  **Appeal Hearing:** Formal hearing with employee, representative,
    > and appeal panel

4.  **Decision:** Appeal panel decision within 15 business days of
    > hearing

5.  **Final Authority:** Board of Directors as final appeals authority
    > for major actions

#### **5.3.2 Appeal Panel Composition**

**Standard Appeal Panel:**

-   **Chair:** Senior manager not involved in original disciplinary
    > decision

-   **HR Representative:** HR manager or designated HR professional

-   **Peer Representative:** Employee representative from same
    > organizational level

-   **Subject Matter Expert:** Individual with expertise relevant to the
    > violation

-   **Legal Counsel:** Legal advisor for complex or significant appeals

**Independence Requirements:**

-   No panel members involved in original investigation or disciplinary
    > decision

-   No direct reporting relationships with appealing employee

-   No conflicts of interest or bias affecting impartial review

-   Training in appeals process and fair hearing procedures

-   Authority to modify, uphold, or reverse original disciplinary
    > decision

## **6. REPORTING MECHANISMS**

### **6.1 Violation Reporting Systems**

#### **6.1.1 Multiple Reporting Channels**

**Direct Reporting:**

-   **Immediate Supervisor:** Primary channel for most policy violations

-   **Department Head:** Alternative for supervisor-related issues

-   **ICT Manager:** Direct reporting for IT security violations

-   **HR Manager:** Human resources violations and employment issues

-   **Compliance Officer:** Regulatory and compliance-related violations

**Anonymous Reporting:**

-   **Anonymous Hotline:** Toll-free telephone reporting system

-   **Anonymous Web Portal:** Secure online reporting system

-   **Anonymous Email:** Dedicated email address for violation reporting

-   **Physical Drop Box:** Secure physical location for written reports

-   **Third-Party Service:** External anonymous reporting service

#### **6.1.2 Reporting Requirements**

**Mandatory Reporting:**

-   All employees must report suspected critical and high-level
    > violations

-   Management personnel must report all violations in their areas

-   IT personnel must report all security-related violations and
    > incidents

-   Contractors and vendors must report violations affecting SAMULCO

-   Failure to report known violations is itself a policy violation

**Reporting Timeline:**

-   **Critical Violations:** Immediate reporting (within 1 hour)

-   **High Violations:** Same day reporting (within 8 hours)

-   **Medium Violations:** Next business day reporting (within 24 hours)

-   **Low Violations:** Within 72 hours or next regular reporting cycle

-   **Suspected Criminal Activity:** Immediate reporting to management
    > and authorities

#### **6.1.3 Reporter Protection**

**Whistleblower Protection:**

-   Protection from retaliation for good faith reporting

-   Anonymous reporting options to protect reporter identity

-   Confidential investigation and handling of reports

-   Clear anti-retaliation policies and enforcement

-   Support and assistance for reporters facing retaliation

**Good Faith Standard:**

-   Protection for reports made in good faith even if ultimately
    > unfounded

-   No penalties for honest mistakes or misunderstandings

-   Clear standards for what constitutes good faith reporting

-   Training on appropriate reporting standards and expectations

-   Investigation of alleged false or malicious reporting

### **6.2 Investigation Procedures**

#### **6.2.1 Investigation Initiation**

**Report Triage:**

-   **Initial Assessment:** Rapid evaluation of report credibility and
    > severity

-   **Priority Assignment:** Classification of investigation priority
    > and urgency

-   **Resource Allocation:** Assignment of appropriate investigation
    > resources

-   **Timeline Establishment:** Target completion date and milestone
    > schedule

-   **Stakeholder Notification:** Appropriate notification of key
    > stakeholders

**Investigation Team Assembly:**

-   **Lead Investigator:** Experienced individual with appropriate
    > authority

-   **Subject Matter Experts:** Personnel with relevant technical or
    > operational expertise

-   **HR Representative:** Human resources professional for employment
    > matters

-   **Legal Counsel:** Legal advisor for complex or high-risk
    > investigations

-   **External Resources:** Outside experts or investigators as needed

#### **6.2.2 Investigation Process**

**Evidence Collection:**

-   **Digital Evidence:** System logs, email records, and electronic
    > documentation

-   **Physical Evidence:** Documents, equipment, and other tangible
    > items

-   **Witness Interviews:** Systematic interviews with relevant
    > witnesses

-   **Expert Analysis:** Technical or specialized analysis of evidence

-   **Chain of Custody:** Proper evidence handling and documentation
    > procedures

**Fair Investigation Standards:**

-   **Presumption of Innocence:** Accused party treated fairly
    > throughout process

-   **Right to Respond:** Opportunity to provide explanation and defense

-   **Impartial Investigation:** Objective and unbiased investigation
    > approach

-   **Confidentiality:** Protection of privacy and reputation during
    > investigation

-   **Documentation:** Comprehensive documentation of investigation
    > process and findings

#### **6.2.3 Investigation Outcomes**

**Investigation Report:**

-   **Executive Summary:** High-level overview of investigation and
    > findings

-   **Factual Findings:** Detailed presentation of facts and evidence

-   **Analysis:** Analysis of evidence and application of relevant
    > policies

-   **Conclusions:** Clear determination of whether violation occurred

-   **Recommendations:** Recommended disciplinary action and preventive
    > measures

**Quality Review:**

-   **Legal Review:** Legal counsel review for accuracy and compliance

-   **Management Review:** Senior management review and approval

-   **Documentation Review:** Verification of complete and accurate
    > documentation

-   **Process Review:** Assessment of investigation process and
    > procedures

-   **Approval Authority:** Final approval by appropriate authority
    > level

### **6.3 Record Keeping and Documentation**

#### **6.3.1 Documentation Standards**

**Comprehensive Documentation:**

-   **Report Details:** Complete record of initial violation report

-   **Investigation Records:** Detailed documentation of investigation
    > process

-   **Evidence Inventory:** Catalog and description of all evidence
    > collected

-   **Interview Summaries:** Written summaries of all interviews
    > conducted

-   **Decision Rationale:** Clear explanation of disciplinary decision
    > reasoning

**Accuracy and Completeness:**

-   **Factual Accuracy:** Verification of all facts and information

-   **Complete Record:** Documentation of all relevant information and
    > activities

-   **Consistent Format:** Standardized documentation format and
    > structure

-   **Timely Documentation:** Prompt documentation while information is
    > fresh

-   **Quality Control:** Review and validation of documentation accuracy

#### **6.3.2 Record Retention and Security**

**Retention Requirements:**

-   **Critical Violations:** Permanent retention in personnel and
    > security files

-   **High Violations:** 7-year retention period for employment and
    > legal purposes

-   **Medium Violations:** 5-year retention period for trend analysis
    > and reference

-   **Low Violations:** 3-year retention period for pattern
    > identification

-   **Investigation Records:** Retention period based on violation
    > severity level

**Security and Confidentiality:**

-   **Secure Storage:** Physical and electronic security for
    > confidential records

-   **Access Controls:** Restricted access to authorized personnel only

-   **Privacy Protection:** Protection of personal information and
    > privacy rights

-   **Legal Compliance:** Compliance with employment law and privacy
    > regulations

-   **Disposal Procedures:** Secure disposal when retention period
    > expires
