## **1. PURPOSE**

This policy establishes the framework for managing third-party service
providers and cloud services at Sta. Ana Multipurpose Cooperative
(SAMULCO). It defines requirements for vendor due diligence, service
level agreements, data processing agreements, risk management, and
ongoing monitoring to ensure secure, compliant, and reliable third-party
services.

## **2. SCOPE**

This policy applies to all:

-   Third-party IT service providers and vendors

-   Cloud service providers and SaaS applications

-   Outsourced IT services and managed services

-   Data processing and hosting services

-   Professional services and consulting arrangements

-   Software vendors and technology suppliers

-   Personnel involved in vendor management and oversight

### **3.2 Risk Classification**

#### **3.2.1 High-Risk Services**

**Characteristics:**

-   Access to sensitive member data or financial information

-   Critical business functions or system dependencies

-   Regulatory compliance or audit implications

-   Single points of failure or limited alternatives

-   Cross-border data transfers or complex jurisdictions

**Examples:**

-   Core banking system providers

-   Cloud hosting for member databases

-   Payment processing services

-   Primary internet and network connectivity

-   Security monitoring and incident response services

**Additional Requirements:**

-   Enhanced due diligence and risk assessment

-   Executive approval and Board notification

-   Comprehensive contract terms and SLAs

-   Regular performance and compliance monitoring

-   Formal business continuity and disaster recovery planning

#### **3.2.2 Medium-Risk Services**

**Characteristics:**

-   Access to internal business data or systems

-   Important but not critical business functions

-   Established alternatives and replacement options

-   Domestic providers with clear regulatory framework

-   Standard industry practices and certifications

**Examples:**

-   Email and collaboration platforms

-   Human resources and payroll systems

-   Document management and storage services

-   Network monitoring and management tools

-   Professional consulting and training services

**Standard Requirements:**

-   Standard due diligence and vendor assessment

-   Management approval and oversight

-   Standard contract terms and service agreements

-   Regular performance reviews and monitoring

-   Basic business continuity and backup procedures

#### **3.2.3 Low-Risk Services**

**Characteristics:**

-   No access to sensitive or confidential data

-   Non-critical support or administrative functions

-   Multiple alternatives and easy replacement

-   Standard commodity services and solutions

-   Minimal regulatory or compliance implications

**Examples:**

-   Office supplies and equipment procurement

-   Facilities and maintenance services

-   Marketing and advertising services

-   Training and development services

-   Standard software licenses and subscriptions

**Minimal Requirements:**

-   Basic vendor verification and assessment

-   Department head approval and oversight

-   Standard purchase agreements and terms

-   Periodic review and performance assessment

-   Basic contract management and renewal procedures

## **6. SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENTS (SLA)**

### **6.1 SLA Framework**

#### **6.1.1 Performance Metrics**

**Availability Metrics:**

-   **System Uptime:** Minimum 99.5% availability during business hours

-   **Planned Downtime:** Maximum 4 hours per month for maintenance

-   **Recovery Time:** Maximum 4 hours for critical system restoration

-   **Performance:** Response times within specified thresholds

-   **Capacity:** Adequate resources for peak usage periods

**Service Quality Metrics:**

-   **Response Time:** Issue acknowledgment within specified timeframes

-   **Resolution Time:** Problem resolution within agreed timelines

-   **First Call Resolution:** Percentage of issues resolved on first
    > contact

-   **Customer Satisfaction:** Regular satisfaction surveys and feedback

-   **Service Delivery:** Meeting agreed service delivery standards

#### **6.1.2 SLA Tiers and Classifications**

**Critical Services (Tier 1):**

-   **Availability:** 99.9% uptime requirement

-   **Response Time:** 15 minutes for critical issues

-   **Resolution Time:** 4 hours for critical issues, 24 hours for high
    > issues

-   **Support Coverage:** 24x7 support with escalation procedures

-   **Penalties:** Significant financial penalties for SLA breaches

**Important Services (Tier 2):**

-   **Availability:** 99.5% uptime requirement

-   **Response Time:** 1 hour for critical issues, 4 hours for high
    > issues

-   **Resolution Time:** 8 hours for critical issues, 48 hours for high
    > issues

-   **Support Coverage:** Business hours with on-call support

-   **Penalties:** Moderate financial penalties for SLA breaches

**Standard Services (Tier 3):**

-   **Availability:** 99.0% uptime requirement

-   **Response Time:** 4 hours for critical issues, 24 hours for high
    > issues

-   **Resolution Time:** 24 hours for critical issues, 5 days for high
    > issues

-   **Support Coverage:** Business hours support only

-   **Penalties:** Service credits and corrective action requirements

### **6.2 SLA Monitoring and Reporting**

#### **6.2.1 Performance Monitoring**

**Monitoring Systems:**

-   Automated monitoring tools and dashboards

-   Real-time alerting and notification systems

-   Performance data collection and analysis

-   Trend analysis and capacity planning

-   Integration with vendor monitoring systems

**Reporting Requirements:**

-   **Real-time:** Performance dashboards and status pages

-   **Daily:** Critical incident reports and resolution status

-   **Weekly:** Performance summary and trend analysis

-   **Monthly:** Comprehensive SLA performance reports

-   **Quarterly:** Service review and improvement planning

#### **6.2.2 SLA Governance**

**Review Processes:**

-   **Monthly:** Performance review and issue discussion

-   **Quarterly:** Comprehensive service review and assessment

-   **Annual:** SLA renewal and improvement planning

-   **Ad-hoc:** Issue escalation and problem resolution

-   **Strategic:** Long-term service planning and optimization

**Escalation Procedures:**

-   **Level 1:** Vendor service manager and SAMULCO technical lead

-   **Level 2:** Vendor account manager and SAMULCO department head

-   **Level 3:** Vendor executive and SAMULCO management team

-   **Level 4:** Executive escalation and contract review

-   **Level 5:** Legal action and contract termination consideration

### **6.3 SLA Remedies and Penalties**

#### **6.3.1 Service Credits**

**Credit Calculation:**

-   Percentage of monthly service fees based on downtime

-   Graduated penalties for different levels of service failure

-   Maximum credit caps and annual limits

-   Credit application and processing procedures

-   Documentation and approval requirements

**Credit Structure Example:**

-   **99.9% - 99.5% availability:** 5% monthly service credit

-   **99.5% - 99.0% availability:** 10% monthly service credit

-   **99.0% - 98.0% availability:** 25% monthly service credit

-   **Below 98.0% availability:** 50% monthly service credit + right to
    > terminate

#### **6.3.2 Performance Improvement**

**Corrective Action Requirements:**

-   Root cause analysis and remediation planning

-   Performance improvement plan development

-   Implementation timeline and milestone tracking

-   Additional monitoring and reporting requirements

-   Success metrics and validation criteria

**Contract Enforcement:**

-   Formal breach notification and cure periods

-   Progressive enforcement and escalation procedures

-   Contract amendment and renegotiation options

-   Legal action and dispute resolution processes
