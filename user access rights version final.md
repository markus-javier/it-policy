**iAccs User Access Rights Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR)**

**[1. Policy Overview]{.smallcaps}**

***1.1. Purpose***

The purpose of this policy is to establish controls and procedures for
**granting**, **modifying**, and **revoking** user access rights to the
SAMULCO\'s information systems, networks, applications, and data. This
ensures the **confidentiality, integrity, and availability** of
organizational resources and minimizes security risks associated with
unauthorized access.

***1.2. Scope***

This policy applies to **all employees, authorized third party
contractors, software, and hardware providers** who require access to
SAMULCO\'s information resources, regardless of location or device used.

***1.3. Relevant Laws***

This policy is part of SAMULCO's compliance to the relevant laws and
regulations such as Data Privacy Act of the Philippines (RA 10173) and
**CDA MC 2023-14** known as **IMPLEMENTING RULES AND REGULATIONS OF
REPUBLIC ACT NO. 11765, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE "FINANCIAL PRODUCTS AND
SERVICES CONSUMER PROTECTION ACT".**

**[2. Core Principles]{.smallcaps}**

*2.1. Principle of Least Privilege as indicated in 3.2. Security
Management Principle of SAMULCO IT Policy 05: Information Security*

Users will be granted only the minimum level of access required to
perform their job duties. Access rights will be role-based and provided
solely for approved business purposes.

*2.2. Segregation of Duties (SoD)*

Where possible, critical tasks will be divided among different
individuals to prevent a single person from having control over an
entire business process.

2.2.1. Establishing and modifications in Access Matrix is managed by
Compliance and ICT.

2.2.2. Operations and Internal Audit shall validate the access matrix in
accordance with the current

job functions.

2.2.3. Access Matrix is duly approved by the Chief Executive Officer or
Chief Operating Officer.

2.2.4. Tasks shall be segregated in following manner: Maker,
Check/Verifier, Approver

*2.3. Accountability*

All access granted must be auditable and traceable to the individual
user. Users are responsible for the security of their credentials and
all actions performed under their account.

**[4. Access Request and Approval Process]{.smallcaps}**

*4.1. Access Request- Newly hired employees*

-   Access requests for new users must be initiated by the Human
    Resource Department as part of the on-boarding process.

-   The request must clearly state the **user\'s role, job function, and
    the specific systems or data** required.

-   All requests must be documented via NEW ACCESS FORM (see Annex 1)
    and Temporary access, Termination of access and Additional access
    via HELPDESK.

*4.2. Approval and Provisioning*

-   Access request form must bear the approval of the CEO or its
    designate prior to provisioning if not covered by a memorandum.

-   The ICT team is responsible for the technical provisioning of access
    once all approval is secured.

-   Any further requests for system access shall be processed and
    evaluated in accordance with **Item 7 -- Access Controls and
    Enforcement**.

**[5. Access Review and Monitoring]{.smallcaps}**

*5.1. Regular Access Review*

Access rights shall be formally reviewed and re-certified monthly by
both the ICT Department and the Compliance Department. Each review must
verify that:

1\. The user's current access level remains appropriate for their job
function.

2\. No unauthorized access has been granted or maintained; and

3\. All processes governing this policy are being properly followed,
tested, and remain compliant.

The Review report will be submitted to CEO from the Compliance.

*5.2. Transfers and Promotions*

When a user changes job roles or departments, their access rights to
systems relevant to the *previous* role must be **reviewed and adjusted
or revoked** within **Five business days** of the transfer. New access
will be requested following the initial access process (Section 4).

*5.3. Disciplinary actions requiring Suspensions*

When a user is serving their suspension, their access rights to systems
are suspended immediately after notification from the Human Resource
Department. Access will be granted upon resumption of work.

*5.4. Long leaves*

When a user is availing leave of absence for three days or more, their
access rights to systems are suspended immediately after notification
from the Human Resource Department. Access will be granted upon
resumption of work.

*5.4. Monitoring*

ICT shall utilize monitoring and logging tools to track user access and
activity. Monthly reports must be submitted to CEO for disposition.
**Suspicious activities** will be reported to CEO for investigation.

**[6. Termination and Revocation of Access]{.smallcaps}**

*6.1. Voluntary or Involuntary Termination*

Upon separation (voluntary or involuntary), all access rights to
SAMULCO's systems, networks, and data must be **immediately revoked**.

*6.2. Off-boarding Procedure*

-   The Human Resource shall inform ICT personnel for the date of
    termination and time in advance.

-   The ICT team is responsible for **disabling the user\'s accounts**
    across all systems and collecting all SAMULCO assets (e.g., laptop,
    access cards).

-   Access will be revoked effectively **immediately at the time of
    termination**.

**[7. Access Controls and Enforcement]{.smallcaps}**

*7.1. Password Requirements*

All users must adhere to the company\'s separate **Password Policy**
which mandates 90 days' expiration of password.

*7.2. Shared and Generic Accounts*

The use of shared or generic accounts is **prohibited** unless
explicitly approved by the CEO for a specific technical requirement
(e.g., a service account). When approved, all activities must still be
auditable.

*7.3. Transfer or sharing of Access*

Access authority is exclusive to an individual. Any transfer of access
is strictly not allowed.

*7.4. Additional access other than current assignment*

7.4.1. The User is required to submit requests via **HELPDESK**
indicating the following information:

a\. Reasonable reason for access

b\. Duration of access

7.4.2. The request must be submitted within two days prior to the grant
of access.

7.4.3. The modifications and additions on the user access rights and
system access are subject for approval of CEO or it's assigned
designates.

7.4.4. Exceptions to this rule are the following:

a\. Teller requests to make bank deposit in the front office module in
IACCS (covered by a memo).The request must be posted in the Group chat
specifically created for this. After the purpose has been served, teller
shall inform ICT for de-activation.

*7.5. Access on the Activity logs*

The following personnel are explicitly declared in this IRR for the
access of the activity logs:

**CEO, Internal Audit Dept, Database Administrator**

This IRR is prepared not in contradiction in the implementation of data
breach protocol.

*7.6. Data Breach Protocols*

7.6.1. Employees are required to report suspected breaches without delay
to Data Privacy Officer.

7.6.2. Once reported the data breach protocols (under Business
continuity plan) must be activated.

**[8. Violations and enforcement]{.smallcaps}**

Any violation of this Policy shall constitute a disciplinary offense and
will be subject to due process. Depending on the gravity of the
infraction, sanctions may include termination of employment.
