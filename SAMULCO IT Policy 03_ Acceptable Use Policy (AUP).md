## **1. PURPOSE**

This Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) establishes guidelines and requirements
for the appropriate, responsible, and secure use of all Information
Technology (IT) resources owned or operated by Sta. Ana Multipurpose
Cooperative (SAMULCO). This policy aims to protect SAMULCO\'s IT
infrastructure, data, and reputation while supporting productive and
effective use of technology resources.

## **2. SCOPE**

This policy applies to all individuals who access, use, or interact with
SAMULCO IT resources, including:

-   Employees (permanent, temporary, contractual)

-   Officers and Board members

-   Contractors and consultants

-   Third-party service providers

-   Visitors and guests (with authorized access)

-   Business partners

## **3. COVERED IT RESOURCES**

This policy applies to all SAMULCO IT resources, including but not
limited to:

### **3.1 Hardware Resources**

-   Desktop computers and laptops

-   Mobile devices (smartphones, tablets)

-   Servers and network equipment

-   Printers and multifunction devices

-   Storage devices and media

-   Communication equipment

### **3.2 Software Resources**

-   Operating systems and applications

-   Cooperative Accounting System (IACCS2013)

-   Database management systems

-   Email and messaging platforms

-   Cloud-based applications

-   Productivity software suites

### **3.3 Network and Communication Resources**

-   Local Area Network (LAN) and wireless networks

-   Internet and intranet access

-   Email systems and accounts

-   Voice over IP (VoIP) services

-   Remote access and VPN connections

-   File sharing and collaboration platforms

### **3.4 Data and Information Resources**

-   Member information and records

-   Financial data and transactions

-   Business and operational data

-   Confidential and proprietary information

-   Backup and archived data

-   Electronic communications

## **4. ACCEPTABLE USE GUIDELINES**

### **4.1 General Principles**

#### **4.1.1 Business Purpose Priority**

-   IT resources are provided primarily for official SAMULCO business
    > purposes

-   Personal use is permitted on a limited basis provided it does not
    > interfere with business operations

-   Business use always takes precedence over personal use

#### **4.1.2 Professional Conduct**

-   Use IT resources in a professional, ethical, and responsible manner

-   Respect intellectual property rights and licensing agreements

-   Maintain confidentiality and privacy of sensitive information

-   Follow all applicable laws, regulations, and cooperative policies

#### **4.1.3 Resource Conservation**

-   Use IT resources efficiently and avoid waste

-   Follow proper procedures for requesting additional resources

-   Report malfunctioning equipment promptly

-   Participate in resource conservation initiatives

### **4.2 Authorized Uses**

#### **4.2.1 Business Operations**

-   **Core Business Functions:** Conducting cooperative business
    > activities and transactions

-   **Communication:** Official business communications with members,
    > partners, and stakeholders

-   **Research and Development:** Job-related research and information
    > gathering

-   **Training and Education:** Participation in authorized training and
    > development programs

-   **Documentation:** Creating, maintaining, and sharing business
    > documents and records

-   **Compliance:** Meeting regulatory and audit requirements

#### **4.2.2 Administrative Functions**

-   **Financial Management:** Processing payments, loans, and other
    > financial transactions

-   **Member Services:** Providing services and support to cooperative
    > members

-   **Human Resources:** Managing employee records and HR processes

-   **Marketing and Outreach:** Promoting cooperative services and
    > community engagement

-   **Planning and Analysis:** Strategic planning and business analysis
    > activities

#### **4.2.3 Limited Personal Use**

Personal use of SAMULCO IT resources is acceptable under the following
conditions:

-   **Minimal Impact:** Does not interfere with work responsibilities or
    > business operations

-   **Reasonable Duration:** Limited time during breaks or non-work
    > hours

-   **Appropriate Content:** Only for lawful and appropriate personal
    > activities

-   **No Cost Impact:** Does not result in additional costs to SAMULCO

-   **Security Compliance:** Follows all security policies and
    > procedures

**Examples of Acceptable Personal Use:**

-   Checking personal email during breaks (using personal accounts)

-   Brief personal phone calls during breaks

-   Accessing news and weather information

-   Educational and professional development activities

-   Emergency personal communications

### **4.3 Email and Communication Guidelines**

#### **4.3.1 Professional Email Use**

-   Use official SAMULCO email accounts for business communications

-   Maintain professional tone and language in all communications

-   Include appropriate subject lines and signatures

-   Follow proper email etiquette and formatting

-   Respect recipient privacy and distribution lists

#### **4.3.2 Email Security Requirements**

-   Do not open suspicious attachments or links

-   Verify sender identity for sensitive requests

-   Use encryption for confidential information when required

-   Report phishing attempts and suspicious emails immediately

-   Follow data classification guidelines for email content

#### **4.3.3 Email Retention and Privacy**

-   Understand that SAMULCO emails are cooperative property

-   Follow retention policies for business communications

-   Expect limited privacy in email communications

-   Use personal email accounts for personal communications

-   Comply with legal hold requirements when applicable

### **4.4 Internet and Web Usage**

#### **4.4.1 Appropriate Internet Use**

-   **Business Research:** Job-related research and information
    > gathering

-   **Professional Development:** Access to training and educational
    > resources

-   **Vendor Communications:** Communicating with business partners and
    > suppliers

-   **Regulatory Compliance:** Accessing regulatory websites and
    > resources

-   **Industry Information:** Staying current with cooperative and
    > financial industry trends

#### **4.4.2 Web Browsing Guidelines**

-   Use judgment and discretion in web browsing activities

-   Avoid excessive personal browsing during work hours

-   Be aware that internet activity is monitored and logged

-   Respect bandwidth limitations and network performance

-   Follow security guidelines for downloading files and software

#### **4.4.3 Social Media Guidelines**

-   **Personal Accounts:** Clearly distinguish personal views from
    > SAMULCO positions

-   **Professional Accounts:** Follow SAMULCO social media and marketing
    > policies

-   **Confidentiality:** Do not share confidential or proprietary
    > information

-   **Respectful Conduct:** Maintain professional conduct and respect
    > for others

-   **Compliance:** Follow all applicable laws and regulations

## **5. PROHIBITED ACTIVITIES**

### **5.1 Strictly Prohibited Uses**

The following activities are absolutely prohibited and may result in
immediate disciplinary action:

#### **5.1.1 Illegal Activities**

-   Any activity that violates Philippine law or international law

-   Copyright infringement and piracy of software, music, movies, or
    > other content

-   Unauthorized access to computer systems or networks (hacking)

-   Identity theft or impersonation

-   Fraud, embezzlement, or other financial crimes

-   Cyberbullying, harassment, or stalking

#### **5.1.2 Security Violations**

-   **Unauthorized Access:** Attempting to access systems or data
    > without authorization

-   **Password Sharing:** Sharing login credentials with others

-   **Malware Introduction:** Introducing viruses, malware, or other
    > malicious software

-   **System Compromise:** Attempting to bypass or disable security
    > controls

-   **Data Theft:** Unauthorized copying or transmission of confidential
    > data

-   **Social Engineering:** Attempting to manipulate others to disclose
    > sensitive information

#### **5.1.3 Inappropriate Content**

-   **Pornographic Material:** Accessing, storing, or distributing
    > sexually explicit content

-   **Hate Speech:** Content that promotes discrimination, hatred, or
    > violence

-   **Offensive Material:** Content that is defamatory, harassing, or
    > intimidating

-   **Gambling:** Online gambling or gaming activities

-   **Illegal Content:** Any content that violates applicable laws

#### **5.1.4 Misuse of Resources**

-   **Personal Gain:** Using SAMULCO resources for personal business or
    > profit

-   **Excessive Use:** Monopolizing systems or consuming excessive
    > bandwidth

-   **Waste:** Intentional waste of IT resources or supplies

-   **Unauthorized Sales:** Selling or transferring SAMULCO property

-   **Resource Theft:** Stealing hardware, software, or other IT assets

### **5.2 Activities Requiring Special Authorization**

The following activities require prior approval from the Chief ICT Officer or
designated authority:

#### **5.2.1 Software Installation**

-   Installing any software not on the approved software list

-   Using personal software on SAMULCO devices

-   Installing browser plugins or extensions

-   Using cloud-based applications not approved by SAMULCO

-   Installing mobile apps on SAMULCO devices

#### **5.2.2 Network Modifications**

-   Connecting personal devices to the SAMULCO network

-   Setting up wireless access points or hotspots

-   Modifying network settings or configurations

-   Using network monitoring or scanning tools

-   Establishing external network connections

#### **5.2.3 Data Handling**

-   Storing SAMULCO data on personal devices or accounts

-   Transmitting confidential data outside the organization

-   Accessing or processing member data beyond job requirements

-   Creating data backups or copies for personal use

-   Sharing data with external parties

## **6. DEVICE-SPECIFIC GUIDELINES**

### **6.1 Desktop and Laptop Computers**

#### **6.1.1 Physical Security**

-   Lock workstations when leaving the desk (Windows + L)

-   Do not leave devices unattended in public areas

-   Report lost or stolen devices immediately

-   Use cable locks for laptops in public spaces

-   Ensure devices are powered off or locked overnight

#### **6.1.2 Software Management**

-   Do not install unauthorized software or applications

-   Keep operating systems and applications updated

-   Use only licensed software provided by SAMULCO

-   Do not disable or modify security software

-   Report software malfunctions to the IT help desk

#### **6.1.3 Data Storage**

-   Store business data on network drives, not local drives

-   Use encryption for sensitive data when required

-   Follow backup procedures for important local data

-   Do not store personal data on business computers

-   Clean up temporary files and downloads regularly

### **6.2 Mobile Devices**

#### **6.2.1 Personal Mobile Device Use**

-   Follow the Bring Your Own Device (BYOD) policy if applicable

-   Install required security applications and configurations

-   Keep devices updated with latest security patches

-   Use strong passwords or biometric authentication

-   Report security incidents or device compromise immediately

#### **6.2.2 SAMULCO-Owned Mobile Devices**

-   Use devices primarily for business purposes

-   Install only approved applications

-   Enable automatic updates for operating system and apps

-   Use strong authentication and enable remote wipe capabilities

-   Report loss or theft within 2 hours

### **6.3 Printing and Document Management**

#### **6.3.1 Printing Guidelines**

-   Print only necessary documents to conserve resources

-   Use duplex printing when appropriate

-   Collect printed documents promptly

-   Ensure confidential documents are not left unattended

-   Use secure printing for sensitive documents

#### **6.3.2 Document Handling**

-   Follow document classification and handling procedures

-   Securely dispose of confidential printed materials

-   Do not leave sensitive documents on desks or in common areas

-   Use appropriate disposal methods for different document types

-   Maintain clean desk policy for security and professionalism

## **7. REMOTE WORK AND BYOD POLICIES**

### **7.1 Remote Work Guidelines**

#### **7.1.1 Home Office Security**

-   Secure home office space and restrict access to family members

-   Use dedicated internet connection for work when possible

-   Ensure family members understand confidentiality requirements

-   Implement physical security measures for work devices

-   Follow all security policies as if working in the office

#### **7.1.2 Remote Access Requirements**

-   Use only approved VPN connections for remote access

-   Connect only from trusted networks and locations

-   Do not use public Wi-Fi for accessing sensitive systems

-   Keep remote access software and credentials secure

-   Log off from all systems at the end of work sessions

### **7.2 Bring Your Own Device (BYOD) Policy**

#### **7.2.1 Device Requirements**

-   Devices must meet minimum security requirements

-   Install required security applications and configurations

-   Keep operating systems and applications updated

-   Use strong passwords or biometric authentication

-   Allow installation of security management software

#### **7.2.2 Data Segregation**

-   Use separate accounts or profiles for personal and business use

-   Do not mix personal and business data on the same device

-   Use approved cloud services for business data synchronization

-   Understand that business data may be remotely wiped if necessary

-   Report device loss or theft immediately

## **8. MONITORING AND ENFORCEMENT**

### **8.1 Monitoring Activities**

SAMULCO reserves the right to monitor IT resource usage for the
following purposes:

#### **8.1.1 Monitoring Scope**

-   **Network Traffic:** Monitoring internet and network usage patterns

-   **Email Communications:** Scanning for security threats and policy
    > compliance

-   **System Performance:** Monitoring system performance and
    > availability

-   **Security Incidents:** Detecting and investigating security threats

-   **Policy Compliance:** Ensuring adherence to IT policies and
    > procedures

#### **8.1.2 Monitoring Methods**

-   Automated security monitoring and alerting systems

-   Network traffic analysis and logging

-   Email content scanning for malware and inappropriate content

-   User activity logging on critical systems

-   Periodic audits and reviews

#### **8.1.3 Privacy Considerations**

-   Monitoring is conducted for business purposes only

-   Personal communications are not targeted for monitoring

-   Monitoring results are kept confidential and secure

-   Access to monitoring data is restricted to authorized personnel

-   Monitoring complies with applicable privacy laws and regulations

### **8.2 Violation Reporting**

#### **8.2.1 Reporting Mechanisms**

-   **IT Help Desk:** Report technical issues and suspected violations

-   **Chief ICT Officer:** Report policy violations and security incidents

-   **Human Resources:** Report employee conduct issues

-   **Anonymous Hotline:** Report violations anonymously if preferred

-   **Email Reporting:** Send reports to designated email addresses

#### **8.2.2 Investigation Process**

-   All reported violations are investigated promptly and thoroughly

-   Investigations are conducted by qualified and authorized personnel

-   Due process is followed in all investigations

-   Confidentiality is maintained throughout the investigation process

-   Results are documented and appropriate actions are taken

## **10. COMPLIANCE AND CONSEQUENCES**

### **10.1 Compliance Expectations**

-   All users must acknowledge reading and understanding this policy

-   Compliance is monitored through various automated and manual methods

-   Regular audits are conducted to ensure policy adherence

-   Violations are investigated and addressed promptly

-   Continuous improvement ensures policies remain effective and
    > relevant

### **10.2 Consequences of Violations**

#### **10.2.1 Progressive Discipline**

-   **First Offense:** Verbal warning and retraining

-   **Second Offense:** Written warning and mandatory training

-   **Third Offense:** Suspension and formal disciplinary action

-   **Serious Violations:** Immediate termination and legal action

#### **10.2.2 Factors Affecting Consequences**

-   **Intent:** Whether the violation was intentional or accidental

-   **Impact:** The severity of consequences resulting from the
    > violation

-   **Frequency:** Whether this is a first-time or repeat violation

-   **Cooperation:** Level of cooperation during investigation

-   **Position:** Employee\'s level of responsibility and access to
    > sensitive data
